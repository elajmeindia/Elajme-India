Top Hospitals in Mumbai for International Patients

November 24, 2026

View:

اقرأ بالعربية

If you are travelling to Mumbai for treatment, start with the specialist and the hospital team that can review your case. This guide compares five Mumbai hospitals by clinical focus and the international patient services they describe. The order is for reference. The right fit depends on your diagnosis, the procedure under consideration and the care you will need after discharge.

Jaslok Hospital & Research Centre

Jaslok brings several medical and surgical departments together at its Mumbai campus. Patients comparing cardiac surgery or neurosurgery teams can ask which consultant would lead their case and which tests can be reviewed before travel.

Selected specialties Its international patient page lists oncology, cardiac care and surgery, orthopaedics and joint replacement, and neurology and neurosurgery.

International patient support The international desk describes online consultations, treatment estimates, and help with visa paperwork and travel. Ask whether an interpreter is available for your appointment and whether transfers or accommodation cost extra.

Kokilaben Dhirubhai Ambani Hospital, Mumbai

Kokilaben’s Mumbai hospital is in Andheri West. Specify the Mumbai campus when requesting a consultation, especially if you are comparing options with the separately listed Navi Mumbai hospital.

Selected specialties The Mumbai campus lists centres for cancer, cardiac sciences, neurosciences, bone and joint care, and rehabilitation. If you expect to need rehabilitation, confirm who would coordinate it and where it would take place.

International patient support The Mumbai international patient page describes help with airport transfers and medical visa invitation letters for eligible cases. Confirm which arrangements your appointment includes and ask about charges that apply to overseas patients.

Nanavati Max Super Speciality Hospital

Nanavati Max is in Vile Parle West. Its specialist institutes span several disciplines, which makes it an option to discuss when your care may involve both a procedure and ongoing specialist review.

Selected specialties Its departments include cancer care, cardiac sciences, neurosciences, orthopaedic and spine care, and kidney sciences and transplantation. Confirm whether the exact service you need is available at this campus.

International patient support Its international patient team describes reviewing medical history, arranging pre-travel consultations and helping from admission through discharge. Ask whether your scans can be reviewed remotely and how to send the original image files.

Sir H. N. Reliance Foundation Hospital & Research Centre

This multispecialty hospital in Girgaon brings imaging, laboratory and nuclear medicine services together on the same campus. If several specialists may be involved, ask how their visits and test results will be coordinated.

Selected specialties Its published clinical areas include cardiac sciences, oncology, neurosciences, gastroenterology and hepatobiliary care, nephro-urology, and orthopaedics and spine care.

International patient support The hospital describes pre-travel consultations, translation and accommodation assistance, and help arranging follow-up and medical records. Confirm interpreter availability and ask how your records will be shared with you after discharge.

S. L. Raheja Hospital

S. L. Raheja is a Fortis associate hospital in Mahim West. Its dedicated diabetic foot unit is worth asking about if your referral concerns a diabetes-related foot problem that may require input from both diabetes and vascular specialists.

Selected specialties The hospital lists diabetes and endocrinology, diabetic foot care, oncology, cardiology, orthopaedics, and nephrology among its specialties. Ask which team would review your referral and whether it can coordinate with your doctor at home.

International patient support Its international patient desk describes pre-travel specialist opinions, appointment scheduling, visa invitation assistance, and interpretation on request. Ask which services need advance booking and whether they appear in the estimate.

How to compare your Mumbai hospital shortlist

Send the same records and questions to each hospital. Ask for the exact campus, the lead specialist, the expected timeline and a written estimate with exclusions. If you will need repeat visits, compare accommodation and transport to that campus. Confirm who will contact you after discharge and what the hospital can share with your local clinician.

Frequently asked questions

Which Mumbai hospital should I shortlist first?

Start with the department that treats your condition and ask who would lead your care. Compare responses from one or two teams about the proposed approach, availability, and follow-up. A hospital’s overall reputation cannot answer whether its team is a fit for your case.

Can a specialist review my reports before I travel?

Ask whether the hospital offers a records review or a video consultation for your specialty. A preliminary opinion can help you plan, but the treating team may need an in-person examination or additional tests before confirming a plan.

What records should I prepare?

Prepare a short medical history, recent reports, imaging files and reports, previous discharge summaries, current medicines and allergies. Ask which formats and translations the hospital needs and how to share them securely before sending sensitive records.

How do I compare treatment estimates?

Request written estimates on the same clinical assumptions, including tests, doctor and hospital fees, implants or medicines, room category and expected stay. Check what extra days, unexpected care, follow-up and nonmedical expenses could add. An estimate can change after assessment.

Can the hospital help with a medical visa or attendant paperwork?

Ask the international desk what supporting documents it can issue and what it needs from you. Check requirements for you and your attendant with the relevant Indian mission or official visa portal. The visa decision rests with the Indian authorities.

Can I arrange Arabic interpretation for my appointments?

Ask the hospital to confirm Arabic interpretation for the specific dates and settings where you need it, including consultations, consent discussions and discharge. Confirm whether it is in person or remote, how to book it and whether there is a fee.

How should I plan where to stay and how long to remain in Mumbai?

Confirm the campus, expected inpatient days, outpatient appointments and when the treating team will reassess fitness to travel. Choose accessible accommodation with practical transport to the hospital and allow flexibility for changes to the care plan.

What should I request before returning home?

Ask for the discharge summary, reports and images, medication instructions, warning signs and a follow-up schedule. Confirm who to contact from abroad, whether remote review is possible and which checks your local doctor should arrange.
