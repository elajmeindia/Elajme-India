Spine care coordination

Spine treatment and surgery coordination in India

Elajme India helps international patients organise spine-related medical records and coordinate specialist review with hospitals in India. Whether surgery is appropriate depends on the diagnosis, symptoms, imaging, previous treatment and individual specialist assessment.

Request a spine case review

Understanding spine treatment

Back or neck symptoms can have many causes. Depending on the individual case, a specialist may discuss non-surgical management, rehabilitation, injections or a surgical option. Elajme India does not select a procedure; we coordinate the case so the treating team can advise the patient.

Specialist assessment

The spine team reviews symptoms, neurological findings, imaging, medical history and previous treatment before advising possible next steps.

Treatment discussion

If an intervention is considered, the treating specialist explains the proposed approach, expected goals, limitations and patient-specific risks.

Recovery and follow-up

Recovery requirements vary by condition and procedure. Rehabilitation, activity guidance and follow-up should be discussed with the treating team.

Important: Not every spine condition requires surgery. Elajme India provides coordination support and does not diagnose, prescribe treatment or guarantee suitability, outcomes or recovery time.

What to share for preliminary review

Where available, send recent consultation notes, MRI or CT reports and image files, X-rays, previous spine surgery or treatment records, relevant neurological reports, current medicines and a brief description of symptoms and their duration.

How Elajme India coordinates your case

1. Share your case

Submit the available reports and imaging information through the Elajme India enquiry process.

2. Case routing

We coordinate the records with an appropriate hospital or specialist team for preliminary review.

3. Medical feedback

We help coordinate available specialist feedback, consultation information and hospital-requested next steps.

4. Visit coordination

If you decide to travel, we can assist with practical coordination connected with appointments and the treatment visit.

Frequently asked questions

Can I request a second opinion before travelling?

Yes. Available reports and imaging can be coordinated for preliminary specialist review. The hospital may request additional information before giving case-specific guidance.

Does a spine enquiry mean I need surgery?

No. A specialist must assess the individual condition. Some patients may be advised non-surgical care or further investigations instead.

Can I receive a treatment estimate?

Where a hospital can provide one, Elajme India can coordinate an indicative estimate after medical review. Final charges depend on the confirmed treatment, investigations, hospital stay and clinical needs.

Does Elajme India recommend a specific operation?

No. Diagnosis and treatment recommendations are the responsibility of qualified medical professionals. Elajme India coordinates communication and practical patient support.
