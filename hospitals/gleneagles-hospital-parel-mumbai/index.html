International patient coordination

Gleneagles Hospital, Parel lists international-patient support such as visa assistance, airport transfers, case management, FRRO assistance and concierge services. Elajme India can help coordinate medical-record submission, hospital communication and specialist consultation requests for patients considering treatment there.

All consultation and treatment enquiries submitted through this page are coordinated through Elajme India only. This page is not a direct hospital booking system.

Medical-visa documentation, travel assistance and related support depend on current hospital and government requirements and are not guaranteed by Elajme India. Doctor association, doctor availability, appointment timing, case acceptance, treatment recommendations and cost estimates are reconfirmed for each patient. Final clinical decisions remain with qualified treating teams, and no treatment outcome is guaranteed.

What happens after you enquire?

Send Elajme India the patient’s medical summary, recent reports, scans and any previous treatment records relevant to the case. We can organise the documents and coordinate a specialist or hospital review request.

After review, the hospital or treating team may request additional investigations before confirming clinical suitability. Where available, Elajme India can then coordinate the next steps such as a consultation or appointment request and communication about the proposed treatment pathway, expected stay and preliminary cost information. These details remain subject to medical review and hospital confirmation.
