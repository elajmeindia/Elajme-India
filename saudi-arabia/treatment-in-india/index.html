Arabic-supported medical coordination for Saudi patients

Planning treatment abroad can involve several steps before a patient is ready to travel. Elajme India acts as a coordination point between the patient and suitable hospitals or specialist teams in India, helping keep case communication, available hospital feedback and practical planning organised.

Case review coordination

We help organise your medical summary, reports and available scans for review by an appropriate hospital or specialist team.

Doctor & hospital communication

Treatment information

Treatment-visit support

If you decide to travel, we can help coordinate appointments and practical treatment-related arrangements such as accommodation and airport transfer when required.

What to send for an initial hospital review

Medical summary

Diagnosis if known, main medical concern, symptoms and relevant medical history.

Recent reports

Recent blood tests, pathology, discharge summaries or specialist notes relevant to the condition.

Imaging

MRI, CT, PET-CT, X-ray or ultrasound reports and images when available and relevant.

Previous treatment

Details of previous surgery, medicines, chemotherapy, radiotherapy or other treatment, where applicable.

The exact documents required depend on the condition and reviewing hospital. Additional tests or an in-person assessment may be requested.

Frequently asked questions

Can I send reports from Saudi Arabia before travelling?

Yes. Elajme India can coordinate available medical records with a hospital or specialist team for preliminary review. The medical team may request additional information or an in-person examination.

Can you coordinate an online consultation?

We can coordinate a remote consultation request when the selected hospital or doctor offers that option. Availability depends on the medical team.

Can I communicate in Arabic?

Yes. Arabic and English communication support is available through Elajme India during the enquiry and coordination journey.

Is a hospital estimate the final treatment cost?

No. An estimate is preliminary and may change after examination, investigations, procedure selection, implant or medicine requirements, complications or length of stay.

Can you help with treatment-related travel planning?

When a patient decides to proceed, Elajme India can coordinate available hospital appointments and practical treatment-visit support. Travel and visa decisions remain subject to the relevant providers and authorities.

Do you guarantee a medical visa or hospital acceptance?

No. Hospital acceptance depends on clinical and administrative review, and visa decisions are made by the relevant authorities. Elajme India can coordinate supporting information but cannot guarantee either outcome.

Elajme India provides international patient coordination. We do not diagnose, prescribe treatment or guarantee medical outcomes, hospital acceptance, final costs or visa approval. Clinical decisions are made by qualified medical professionals.
