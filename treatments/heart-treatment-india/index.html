Understanding heart treatment

The appropriate treatment depends on the heart condition, symptoms, test results, previous treatment and the patient's overall health. A qualified cardiology or cardiac-surgery team must assess the individual case and recommend the treatment plan.

Medical management

Some heart conditions are managed with medicines and lifestyle measures under a clinician's supervision.

Coronary angioplasty and stent

Angioplasty may be considered for narrowed or blocked coronary arteries after specialist assessment.

Coronary bypass surgery

Bypass surgery may be considered for selected patients depending on the pattern and severity of coronary artery disease.

Heart valve assessment

Specialist evaluation helps determine whether monitoring, medication or a procedure may be appropriate.

Heart rhythm assessment

Rhythm disorders require individual evaluation before a medical or procedural treatment plan is recommended.

Cardiac surgery review

Where surgery is being considered, the treating team reviews reports, imaging, risks and the patient's overall condition.

This page provides general coordination information and is not medical advice. Diagnosis and treatment decisions are made by qualified medical professionals.

What to share for hospital review

Relevant documents may include a recent medical summary, ECG, echocardiogram, angiography or other cardiac reports if available, previous procedure records, current medicines and recent blood-test reports. Share only documents relevant to your enquiry.
