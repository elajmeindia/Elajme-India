Important information about the role and limits of Elajme India's international patient coordination services.

1. Our role

Elajme India provides healthcare facilitation and international patient coordination for people considering treatment in India. Coordination may include communication with hospitals, organising case-review information, appointments, hospital-provided estimates and practical treatment-visit support.

Medical disclaimer: Elajme India is not a hospital, clinic, doctor or medical provider. We do not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, determine clinical suitability or provide medical advice.

Information on this website is general and intended for informational and coordination purposes. It is not a substitute for consultation, diagnosis or advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

3. Doctors, hospitals and medical opinions

Directory listings do not imply ranking, endorsement, formal partnership, guaranteed availability or suitability for an individual patient. Medical opinions, diagnoses, treatment plans and clinical recommendations are the responsibility of relevant qualified healthcare professionals or institutions.

4. Treatment estimates and costs

Any estimate or cost information provided by a hospital may change after examination, investigations or changes in the treatment plan, length of stay, complications, hospital policy or other circumstances. Patients should confirm financial information with the relevant provider before proceeding.

5. Treatment outcomes

No treatment result, recovery period, clinical outcome or complication-free course can be guaranteed by Elajme India.

6. Visa, travel and logistics

We may assist with practical medical-travel coordination, but visa, immigration and entry decisions are controlled by relevant government authorities. Travel, accommodation, airlines, transport and other independent providers operate under their own terms and policies.

7. Patient responsibility

Patients and families are responsible for providing accurate relevant information, asking appropriate questions and reviewing medical, financial, visa and travel information before deciding to proceed.

8. Third parties

Hospitals, doctors, hotels, transport providers, airlines, messaging platforms and other third parties are independent. Elajme India does not control their professional decisions, availability, policies or performance.

9. Transplants

Transplant eligibility, donor suitability and required medical, hospital, legal and regulatory approvals are determined by qualified transplant teams and relevant authorities. Elajme India does not determine or guarantee eligibility, acceptance or approval.

10. Emergencies

Our website and coordination channels are not emergency medical services. Seek immediate local medical assistance if a patient has an emergency or urgent deterioration.
