How we support the enquiry

Medical case coordination

We help organise relevant reports and imaging information for specialist review.

Specialist communication

Visit planning

If the patient decides to proceed, we can assist with practical treatment-visit coordination.

Neurological conditions vary widely. Diagnosis and treatment recommendations must come from qualified specialists after reviewing the individual case.

Preparing records for review

Useful records may include recent consultation notes, MRI or CT reports and images, pathology reports where applicable, previous surgery or treatment records and a current medication list.
