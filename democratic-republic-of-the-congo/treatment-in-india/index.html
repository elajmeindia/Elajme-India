Medical treatment in India for patients from Democratic Republic of the Congo

If you are arranging an overseas referral from Kinshasa, Lubumbashi or another city in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Elajme India can ask an Indian hospital to review your records, clarify specialist options and issue a preliminary estimate where available.

Records for a hospital review from Kinshasa

Prepare the clinical timeline

List the date of diagnosis, previous procedures and current care. Ask your referring clinician to explain why an overseas assessment is needed.

Organise French reports

Keep original comptes rendus, relevant laboratory or pathology results and original images. Ask which documents need English translation and how the department wants files delivered.

Send records with consent

Begin with the enquiry form or ask for submission instructions on WhatsApp. Obtain permission before sharing a relative’s medical records.

Request specialist review

Clarify testing and treatment

Ask what the specialist can assess remotely and whether the hospital expects repeat tests before confirming a procedure.

Request an itemised quotation

Compare diagnostics, consultant and procedure fees, room charges, medicines and any limits or exclusions. Ask about additional care and the refund terms for deposits.

Confirm admission and local follow-up

Check the date and documents for admission, length of recovery near the hospital and options for follow-up with your clinician in the DRC.

Ask the hospital for a written estimate in its billing currency. Allow separately for any internal travel to your departure airport, international flights, visas, accommodation, local transport and attendant costs. Check international payment limits with your bank before making a deposit.

Medical visa and travel from the Democratic Republic of the Congo

Check the correct visa route

Ask the Indian Embassy in Kinshasa which medical and attendant applications apply to your passport. Use the official regular visa portal and confirm its requirements for the hospital letter and supporting documents with the mission.

Yellow fever and transit

India’s health guidance lists the Democratic Republic of the Congo among yellow fever risk countries. Verify certificate requirements for the patient and companion, including any transit stops, with the airline and Indian arrival health authority. Ask a travel health clinician about eligibility.

Book around the confirmed appointment

Before choosing connections from Kinshasa or another departure city, confirm the Indian hospital campus and date. Check transit documents, any change of airport and accessible transport. Confirm accommodation within practical reach of the hospital.

Use the official Indian regular visa portal and confirm submission procedures and required documents for the Medical Visa and Medical Attendant Visa with the embassy, according to your passport.

Questions before travelling from Kinshasa

Do I need to translate my French reports first?

Ask the reviewing department which documents it requires in English. Keep the originals and confirm whether interpretation is available if needed.

Can I get a second opinion before travelling from Kinshasa?

We can ask the hospital about a document review or remote appointment. The specialist decides whether an in-person assessment is necessary.

How can I budget for treatment?

Use a written hospital estimate and a separate travel budget. Verify any limits on included tests, room days and medicines.

Can a family member accompany me?

Check the visa route for a Medical Attendant with the Indian mission in Kinshasa and ask the hospital what documents it can provide.

When can I fly back to the DRC?

Ask for expected inpatient and local recovery time and a follow-up appointment. The treating clinician should assess fitness for the return journey.

How can my local doctor continue my care?

Gather your discharge summary, results and instructions, and ask whether the Indian team can send a copy in a language your local clinician can use.
