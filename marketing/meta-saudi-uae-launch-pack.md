# Elajme India — Saudi Arabia + UAE Meta/Instagram → WhatsApp Launch Pack

## Purpose
Ready-to-use launch material for the first two GCC Meta markets: Saudi Arabia and UAE. Elajme India is positioned only as a medical-travel and patient-coordination service. Hospitals and doctors make clinical decisions. Never promise treatment outcome, hospital acceptance, visa approval, exact price, or cure.

## Conversion destination
Primary CTA: WhatsApp Elajme India only: +91 74889 55154.
Secondary route where appropriate: the relevant Elajme country/treatment landing page before WhatsApp.

## Campaign naming
- META_KSA_AR_WA_PROSPECTING
- META_KSA_EN_WA_PROSPECTING
- META_UAE_AR_WA_PROSPECTING
- META_UAE_EN_WA_PROSPECTING

Ad-set convention: COUNTRY_LANGUAGE_TREATMENT_INTENT
Example: KSA_AR_CARDIAC_INTENT

Creative convention: COUNTRY_LANGUAGE_TREATMENT_FORMAT_ANGLE_V01
Example: KSA_AR_CARDIAC_STATIC_COORDINATION_V01

## Treatment clusters
Use separate ad sets/creative groups for:
1. Cancer care
2. Heart/cardiac care
3. Kidney transplant
4. Liver transplant
5. Knee replacement
6. Neurosurgery
7. Spine surgery
8. Urology
9. Gastroenterology

Do not imply that Meta knows or has inferred a viewer's medical condition. Avoid copy such as “Do you have cancer?” or “Your kidney disease”. Use neutral service-led language such as “Seeking information about cancer treatment options in India?”

# Creative concepts

## Creative 1 — Coordination journey
Format: 4:5 feed static/carousel and 9:16 Stories/Reels adaptation.
Message hierarchy: Share reports → Case coordination → Consultation/appointment planning → Travel support.
Visual direction: clean Elajme branding, India/GCC travel context, professional hospital/travel imagery. Avoid graphic clinical imagery and before/after claims.

## Creative 2 — Arabic support
Headline concept: Arabic-speaking coordination for treatment planning in India.
Show: WhatsApp conversation motif, report/document iconography, appointment calendar and travel-support cues. Do not display real patient reports or identifiable medical data.

## Creative 3 — Treatment-specific
One treatment per creative. Use neutral specialty imagery and the treatment name. Message should focus on coordination, case review and next-step planning, not medical efficacy.

# UTM structure
Meta country landing example:

Treatment example:
?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=uae_en_cardiac&utm_content=cardiac_static_v01

Use campaign names that contain no patient-identifying or medical-report information. GA4 receives campaign/traffic attribution only, never patient name, phone, diagnosis, report contents or WhatsApp message contents.

# Initial testing structure
For each market:
- Arabic general coordination ad set
- English general coordination ad set
- treatment-specific tests only after sufficient budget/data, prioritizing the strongest commercial treatment categories
- 2–3 materially different creatives per active ad set
- test copy/creative, not tiny cosmetic variations

Optimize toward qualified enquiries, not raw WhatsApp opens.

# Lead-quality review
Track outside GA4:
- country
- source/campaign
- treatment/specialty
- reports available: yes/no
- genuine treatment intent
- case stage
- next follow-up date
- qualified/unqualified
- consultation progression
- travel progression
- converted/closed

Do not place patient names, phone numbers, diagnoses or medical report details into GA4 campaign parameters.

# Launch checklist
- Meta Business/Ads account ready
- Elajme-owned WhatsApp destination verified
- Saudi and UAE country pages tested on mobile
- treatment landing routes tested before using treatment-specific ads
- UTMs tested and visible in analytics attribution
- WhatsApp CTA opens only Elajme India
- Arabic creative proofread in final artwork
- no guaranteed outcome/cure/cost/visa/acceptance language
- no sensitive-health personal-attribute wording
- privacy and medical-disclaimer pages accessible
- lead follow-up pipeline ready before spend begins

## Decision rule
Launch Saudi Arabia and UAE as separate markets and Arabic/English as separate ad sets/campaign components. Review qualified-lead quality before expanding budget or copying the campaign to Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain.
