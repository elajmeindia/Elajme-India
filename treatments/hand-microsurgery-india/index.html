Hand and microsurgery in India for international patients

Elajme India helps international patients coordinate hand surgery and microsurgery enquiries with specialist teams in India. Depending on the injury or condition, a surgeon may review hand function, nerve or tendon involvement, imaging and previous treatment before advising next steps.

Hand, nerve and microsurgery assessment

Specialist assessment

The specialist team reviews symptoms, diagnosis, previous treatment, relevant investigations and medical history for hand, peripheral nerve and microsurgical conditions.

Condition-specific care

Depending on the location and severity of the injury, and whether nerves, tendons or blood vessels are affected, the hand surgeon may recommend further investigations or input from reconstructive surgery or rehabilitation specialists.

Treatment and follow-up

The surgical team discusses repair, non-surgical care and rehabilitation options based on the injury and hand function, with follow-up and recovery planning tailored to the individual assessment.

Elajme India provides international patient coordination and general information. We do not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment or guarantee treatment acceptance or outcomes.

What to share for an initial review

Useful records may include the diagnosis, recent specialist notes, blood tests, imaging or other relevant investigations, previous treatment and current medicines.

1. Send available records

3. Confirm next steps

Investigations, treatment options, timing, eligibility and estimates remain subject to the treating provider’s review.

Preparing for a hand and microsurgery review

What the clinical team reviews

Hand and microsurgery planning considers injury or disease of bone, tendon, nerve, vessel and soft tissue, together with hand function, sensation, circulation, imaging and previous repair or rehabilitation.

Records that can help

Send X-ray, CT or MRI reports and images where relevant, nerve-conduction studies if available, injury and operation records, photographs when appropriate, rehabilitation notes and current medicines.

The treating team confirms diagnosis, suitability, investigations and treatment after individual review. Elajme India coordinates case submission and communication and does not replace medical advice.
