CANCER TREATMENT COORDINATION IN INDIA

Cancer treatment in India for international patients

Elajme India helps international patients and families coordinate cancer-care enquiries with hospitals and oncology teams in India. We support case review, appointments, treatment estimates and practical travel planning.

Start an enquiry on WhatsApp

Cancer treatment is individual to each patient

The appropriate treatment depends on factors such as the type of cancer, how advanced it is, previous treatment and the patient's overall health. A qualified oncology team must assess the individual case and recommend the treatment plan.

Surgery

Surgery may be used for many solid tumours depending on the cancer, its location and extent.

Chemotherapy

Chemotherapy uses medicines to treat cancer and may be used alone or with other treatments.

Radiation therapy

Radiation therapy may be recommended alone or in combination with other treatment approaches.

Immunotherapy

Some cancers may be treated with therapies that help the immune system respond to cancer.

Targeted therapy

Targeted therapies are suitable only for selected cases based on the characteristics of the cancer.

Stem cell transplant

Stem cell transplantation may be considered for selected blood cancers after specialist assessment.

This page provides general coordination information and is not medical advice. Diagnosis and treatment decisions are made by qualified medical professionals.

What to share for hospital review

Relevant documents may include a recent medical summary, pathology or biopsy reports, imaging reports and images if available, previous treatment records, current medicines and recent blood-test reports. Share only documents relevant to your enquiry.
