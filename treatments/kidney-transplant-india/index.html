Kidney transplant coordination

Kidney transplant in India for international patients

Elajme India helps international patients coordinate kidney-transplant enquiries with transplant hospitals and specialist teams in India. We can help organise patient and potential-donor information, request preliminary hospital review and coordinate practical next steps when the transplant team responds.

Request kidney transplant coordination

Kidney transplant requires individual medical and legal assessment

A transplant programme must assess the recipient, the proposed donor where applicable, medical suitability, required investigations and documentation. Acceptance and the final pathway depend on the hospital, qualified transplant professionals and applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Recipient assessment

The transplant team may review kidney-disease history, dialysis information, previous treatment, investigations, other medical conditions and overall fitness for transplantation.

Potential-donor assessment

Where a living donor is proposed, the donor requires independent medical evaluation and documentation according to the transplant programme and applicable requirements.

Hospital and regulatory process

The hospital will advise which medical, relationship, identity and other documents are required and whether additional approvals are necessary for the individual case.

Important: Elajme India does not determine transplant eligibility, donor suitability, legal approval or organ availability, and does not guarantee hospital acceptance, transplantation or outcomes.

What to share for preliminary hospital review

Requirements vary by case and hospital. For an initial enquiry, available information can help the transplant team understand the case before advising the next steps.

Patient records

A recent medical summary, kidney-function reports, dialysis details if applicable, imaging, laboratory reports, current medicines and records of previous treatment.

Potential-donor information

Basic donor details and available medical records can be shared when requested. The hospital will specify the formal donor evaluation and documentation required.

Identity and relationship documents

The transplant hospital may request identity, relationship and other supporting documents. Exact requirements should be confirmed directly through the hospital process.

How Elajme India coordinates your enquiry

1. Share available information

Submit the patient's diagnosis, medical records and relevant potential-donor information available at this stage.

2. Transplant-team review request

We coordinate the enquiry with an appropriate hospital transplant programme for preliminary guidance.

3. Receive case-specific requirements

When the hospital responds, we help communicate the requested investigations, documents, consultation details and next steps.

4. Plan the treatment visit

If the hospital advises the case may proceed, Elajme India can assist with appointment, travel and practical coordination around the treatment visit.

Frequently asked questions

Can Elajme India confirm whether I am eligible for transplant?

No. Eligibility can only be determined by the transplant hospital and qualified medical team after reviewing the patient and completing the required assessment.

Can you confirm whether my donor will be accepted?

No. Donor suitability requires independent medical assessment and the required hospital and regulatory process. Elajme India can coordinate communication and documentation, but does not approve donors.

Can you guarantee a kidney transplant in India?

No. Transplantation depends on medical suitability, donor circumstances, hospital acceptance and applicable requirements. No transplant or outcome can be guaranteed.
