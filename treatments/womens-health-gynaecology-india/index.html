Gynaecology conditions and specialist care

Gynaecological conditions

Specialist assessment may be relevant for menstrual disorders, fibroids, ovarian or uterine conditions, pelvic symptoms and other gynaecological concerns.

Gynaecological surgery

When surgery is being considered, the treating team determines whether a procedure is appropriate and which surgical approach may be suitable after evaluation.

Preparing for a gynaecology review

What the clinical team reviews

Gynaecology review depends on the patient’s symptoms, reproductive and menstrual history, imaging, previous procedures and the suspected condition. Benign gynaecology, complex surgery and cancer-related concerns may require different specialist teams.

Records that can help

Send recent gynaecology notes, ultrasound or MRI reports, cervical or pathology results when relevant, previous surgery records, menstrual or reproductive history and a current medication list.

The treating team confirms diagnosis, suitability, investigations and treatment after individual review. Elajme India coordinates case submission and communication and does not replace medical advice.
