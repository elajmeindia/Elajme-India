Understanding IVF and fertility treatment options

Specialist consultation

A fertility specialist may review medical history, previous treatment and available investigations before advising next steps.

IVF and assisted reproduction

Suitability for IVF or another assisted-reproduction approach can only be determined after appropriate specialist assessment.

Further investigations

The treating team may request additional tests before confirming a treatment plan, timeline or estimate.

Elajme India provides coordination and general information. We do not diagnose infertility, prescribe treatment, promise pregnancy or guarantee treatment outcomes.

What to share for an initial review

Depending on the case, useful records may include a medical summary, previous fertility-treatment records, relevant laboratory reports, imaging or procedure reports and details of current medicines.

1. Send available records

3. Confirm next steps

Consultation availability, investigations, treatment options, timing and estimates remain subject to the treating provider's review.

Preparing for fertility and IVF review

What the clinical team reviews

Fertility assessment is individual to the patient or couple and may include reproductive history, ovarian reserve, semen analysis, uterine or tubal assessment and previous fertility treatment.

Records that can help

Send previous fertility-clinic notes, hormone and ovarian-reserve tests, semen analysis, ultrasound or tubal studies, records of previous IVF cycles or procedures and relevant medical or surgical history.

The treating team confirms diagnosis, suitability, investigations and treatment after individual review. Elajme India coordinates case submission and communication and does not replace medical advice.
