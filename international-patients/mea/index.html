Arabic & English international patient coordination

Planning medical treatment in India from the GCC or overseas?

Elajme India gives patients and families one coordination point for sharing medical records, communicating with hospitals and specialist teams, receiving available treatment information and planning the practical steps of a treatment visit to India.

Share Your Medical Case
Serving enquiries from Saudi Arabia, UAE, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and other international markets. Hospital acceptance and treatment recommendations depend on medical review.

What Elajme India can coordinate

Medical record review

We organise the available reports, scans and medical history for review by an appropriate hospital or specialist team.

Hospital and doctor communication

We coordinate consultation requests, available appointments and communication connected with the patient enquiry.

Treatment information and estimates

Where provided by the hospital, we share medical feedback, proposed next steps and indicative estimates so the patient can review them before deciding.

Travel and local coordination

For patients proceeding to India, we can help coordinate treatment-related appointments and practical support such as airport transfer, accommodation and local logistics where requested.

Your journey with Elajme India

1. Share your case

Tell us the medical concern, country of residence and preferred communication language, and upload the reports you already have.

2. Case coordination

We coordinate the information with a suitable hospital or specialist team for review.

3. Review the response

We share available hospital feedback, consultation information and estimates. You decide whether you wish to proceed.

4. Plan the visit

If you proceed, we coordinate appointments and help organise the practical treatment journey in India.

For patients from the GCC

What to send for an initial hospital review

Medical summary

A short description of the diagnosis or current medical concern, symptoms and previous treatment.

Recent reports

Relevant laboratory reports, imaging reports, pathology or biopsy results and discharge summaries where applicable.

Scans and images

MRI, CT, PET, angiography or other imaging files may be useful depending on the condition.

Current treatment details

Include current medicines, previous procedures and any important medical conditions or allergies known to you.

Do not delay urgent or emergency care while waiting for an international review. The receiving medical team may request additional records or tests before providing an opinion.

Frequently asked questions

Does Elajme India choose my treatment?

No. Diagnosis, investigations and treatment recommendations are made by qualified medical professionals after reviewing the individual case.

Can I get a hospital opinion before travelling?

Where a hospital or specialist offers remote case review, we can coordinate submission of the available records and share the response received.

Is a treatment estimate final?

No. An estimate provided before travel is generally indicative and may change after examination, additional tests, treatment changes or complications.

Can you guarantee a medical visa?

No. We can provide coordination and general guidance, but visa approval is decided by the relevant authorities and cannot be guaranteed.
