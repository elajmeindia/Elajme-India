Fertility care coordination

IVF and fertility treatment in India for international patients

Elajme India helps international patients coordinate fertility-care enquiries with hospitals and qualified specialist teams in India. The appropriate evaluation and treatment pathway depends on the individual patient or couple and is determined by the treating team.

Request coordination through Elajme India

Fertility care is individual

Specialist consultation

A fertility specialist may review medical history, previous treatment and available investigations before advising next steps.

IVF and assisted reproduction

Suitability for IVF or another assisted-reproduction approach can only be determined after appropriate specialist assessment.

Further investigations

The treating team may request additional tests before confirming a treatment plan, timeline or estimate.

Elajme India provides coordination and general information. We do not diagnose infertility, prescribe treatment, promise pregnancy or guarantee treatment outcomes.

What to share for an initial review

Depending on the case, useful records may include a medical summary, previous fertility-treatment records, relevant laboratory reports, imaging or procedure reports and details of current medicines. The specialist team decides what information or testing is required.

1. Send available records

Share the main concern and relevant available medical documents with Elajme India.

2. Coordinate specialist review

We can coordinate submission to an appropriate hospital or fertility team for preliminary review.

3. Confirm next steps

Consultation availability, investigations, treatment options, timing and estimates remain subject to the treating provider's review.
