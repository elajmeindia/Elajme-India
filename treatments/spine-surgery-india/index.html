Spine care

Spine treatment and surgery coordination in India

Elajme India coordinates spine-related case reviews with hospitals and specialist teams in India. The appropriate investigation or treatment depends on the individual diagnosis, symptoms, imaging and specialist assessment.

Request coordination through Elajme India

How we support your enquiry

Medical case review

We help organise relevant reports and imaging information for specialist review.

Hospital coordination

We coordinate hospital communication, consultation requests and available appointment information.

Treatment-visit planning

If the patient proceeds, we can assist with practical arrangements connected with the visit to India.

Not every spine condition requires surgery. Elajme India does not diagnose conditions or recommend a particular procedure. Clinical decisions are made by qualified medical professionals.

Information for preliminary review

A specialist may request consultation notes, MRI or CT reports and images, previous surgery records and a current medication list.
