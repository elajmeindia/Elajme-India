Oman → India patient coordination

Medical treatment in India for patients from Oman

Elajme India helps patients and families from Oman coordinate medical-case review with hospitals and specialist teams in India, consultation requests, available treatment estimates, appointments and practical treatment-visit planning. Arabic and English communication support is available throughout the journey.

Request Case Review

What to share before planning travel

Medical summary

Share the diagnosis or main medical concern, symptoms, previous treatment and relevant medical history.

Reports & imaging

Include recent laboratory results, discharge summaries, pathology reports and MRI, CT, X-ray or other imaging where relevant.

Current medicines

Provide a current medication list and important medical conditions or allergies that may affect specialist review.

Previous procedures

Include available records of previous surgery, procedures or major treatment related to the current condition.

Complete records can help the receiving medical team understand the enquiry, but additional tests or an in-person assessment may still be required in India.

How Elajme India coordinates your Oman-to-India journey

1. Share your case

Send your medical concern and available records through Elajme India.

2. Specialist or hospital review

We coordinate the enquiry with an appropriate hospital or specialist team for case-specific review.

3. Review available feedback

We share available medical feedback, consultation information and preliminary hospital estimates when provided.

4. Coordinate the visit

If you choose to proceed, we help coordinate appointments and practical treatment-related travel and local requirements.

Support available through Elajme India

Arabic & English communication

Communication support can help patients and families understand the coordination steps and information received from India.

Remote consultation requests

Where a hospital or doctor offers it, we can coordinate a request for an online consultation before travel. Availability depends on the medical provider.

Hospital estimates

We can request available preliminary estimates. Final costs may change after clinical assessment, investigations, procedure details, implants, medicines or length of stay.

Travel & local coordination

When required, we can assist with treatment-related appointment planning and practical arrangements such as stay, airport transfer and local coordination.

Frequently asked questions

Can my reports be reviewed before I travel from Oman?

Often, hospitals can review available records before travel. Whether the records are sufficient and whether further tests are required are decided by the medical team.

Can I receive a treatment cost before travelling?

A hospital may provide a preliminary estimate after reviewing the case. It should not be treated as a guaranteed final bill.

Can Elajme India arrange an online doctor consultation?

We can coordinate a request where the selected medical provider offers remote consultation. Appointment availability is controlled by the provider.

Does Elajme India issue medical visas?

No. We can provide coordination guidance and help with available hospital documents, but visa decisions are made only by the relevant authorities.
