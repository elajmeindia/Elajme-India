# Elajme India — International Patient Lead Follow-up Pipeline

## Purpose
This is the operating structure for handling international patient enquiries generated by the website, organic search, Google Ads, Meta campaigns and WhatsApp. All patient communication and consultation coordination remains through Elajme India.

## Pipeline stages
1. **New Lead** — enquiry received but not yet qualified.
2. **Contacted** — first response sent or call attempted.
3. **Reports Awaited** — patient has been asked for the relevant available medical reports/information.
4. **Reports Received** — available documents received and ready for coordination.
5. **Case Sent** — case sent by Elajme India to an appropriate hospital/medical team for review.
6. **Hospital Response** — response, consultation availability or preliminary information received from the hospital.
7. **Patient Follow-up** — hospital information shared and patient decision/questions pending.
8. **Consultation** — consultation request/appointment is being coordinated or completed.
9. **Travel Planning** — patient has chosen to proceed and practical treatment-visit coordination is underway.
10. **Converted** — patient has proceeded through Elajme India to the coordinated treatment journey.
11. **Closed / Not Proceeding** — patient declined, became unreachable after reasonable follow-up, chose another route, or the case cannot proceed.

## Minimum operational fields
Keep these in the operational lead system, not GA4:

- Lead ID
- Enquiry date/time
- Patient country
- Preferred language
- Treatment/specialty category
- Lead source: Organic / Google Ads / Meta / Direct / Referral / Other
- Campaign name or UTM campaign when available
- Landing page
- Current pipeline stage
- Assigned coordinator
- Last contact date
- Next follow-up date
- Follow-up count
- Hospital/medical team contacted
- Hospital response status
- Consultation status
- Travel-planning status
- Outcome
- Internal notes

Medical reports, diagnoses, phone numbers, names and other patient-identifying or medical information must not be sent to GA4. Store them only in an appropriately controlled operational system when needed for patient coordination.

## Follow-up cadence
Use judgement based on urgency and patient preference. A practical default for non-emergency enquiries is:

- New lead: acknowledge as soon as reasonably possible during operating hours.
- Reports awaited: first reminder after 24 hours when appropriate.
- Still awaiting: second reminder around 72 hours later.
- Case sent to hospital: monitor internally rather than repeatedly asking the patient for the same information.
- Hospital response received: contact the patient promptly and set a clear next-follow-up date.
- Patient deciding: follow up according to the patient's stated timeline rather than excessive messaging.

Do not use this workflow for emergencies. Patients describing emergency or rapidly worsening symptoms should be advised to seek urgent local medical care rather than wait for website coordination.

## Source attribution
Where campaign attribution is available, retain:

- utm_source
- utm_medium
- utm_campaign
- utm_content
- utm_term

Recommended normalized source labels:

- google / cpc → Google Ads
- facebook or instagram / paid_social → Meta
- organic search → Organic
- direct → Direct
- partner/referral → Referral

The website analytics layer tracks privacy-safe conversion/source metadata. The operational pipeline may retain the lead's source alongside the patient record so marketing performance can be connected to actual qualified enquiries without sending medical or personally identifying data to analytics platforms.

## Lead quality
Suggested operational classification:

- **Qualified** — genuine treatment need, relevant country/market, sufficient intent to continue coordination.
- **Needs information** — potentially valid but reports/details are insufficient.
- **Unqualified** — spam, irrelevant request, vendor solicitation or enquiry outside service scope.

Do not classify clinical suitability. That decision belongs to qualified medical professionals/hospitals.

## Core performance metrics
Track monthly:

- total enquiries
- qualified enquiries
- qualified enquiry rate
- enquiries by country
- enquiries by treatment category
- enquiries by source/campaign
- reports received rate
- cases sent for hospital review
- hospital responses received
- consultations coordinated
- patients entering travel planning
- converted patients
- average first-response time

Primary business KPI: **qualified international patient enquiries managed through Elajme India**.

## Ownership rule
Every open lead must have:

1. one current stage,
2. one owner/coordinator,
3. a last-contact date, and
4. either a next-follow-up date or a documented reason why no follow-up is required.

This prevents genuine enquiries from being lost between WhatsApp, phone and email conversations.
