Allergy and clinical immunology coordination

Allergy and clinical immunology in India

If you are seeking allergy or clinical immunology care in India, Elajme India can help coordinate your enquiry with a relevant specialist team. The appropriate doctor depends on whether your concern is allergic, respiratory, dermatological, autoimmune or another immune-related condition.

Verified clinical-immunology pathway

For immune-mediated rheumatological and clinical-immunology cases, Elajme India can coordinate review with Dr. Rajiva Gupta, Chairman of Rheumatology and Immunology at Medanta – The Medicity, Gurugram. Medanta identifies him as head of its Division of Rheumatology and Clinical Immunology.

Clinical immunology

Immune-mediated systemic and rheumatological conditions can require clinical-immunology assessment and multidisciplinary review.

Allergy cases

Allergic disease may instead require an allergy, pulmonology, ENT, dermatology or paediatric specialist depending on symptoms and age. Elajme India does not present a rheumatologist as the universal provider for every allergy case.

Individual matching

Provider availability, hospital association and suitability are reconfirmed for each case after review of the medical records.

Records useful for review

Share previous allergy or immunology notes, immune-function or allergy testing where available, blood results, details of significant reactions or recurrent infections, suspected triggers, current medicines and relevant respiratory, skin or rheumatology records.

Elajme India provides coordination and general information. Diagnosis, testing and treatment decisions remain with the treating medical team.
