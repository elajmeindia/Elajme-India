COMMON QUESTIONS

What you may want to know before getting started.

Find answers to common questions about treatment coordination, costs, medical visas and sharing your medical information.

Is there a fee for the initial enquiry?

You can contact us for an initial discussion about your treatment needs and the coordination process. If any service fees apply, they will be clearly explained and agreed with you in advance.

How are treatment costs and expected outcomes determined?

Treatment plans and cost estimates are provided by the hospital or treating medical team following a clinical assessment of the patient’s condition and medical information. The final cost may vary depending on the treatment required, investigations, length of stay and other clinical factors. Treatment outcomes also vary from patient to patient and cannot be guaranteed.

Can you help with a medical visa?

We can help coordinate information and practical steps. Visa eligibility and approval are determined by the relevant authorities; please check official guidance.

Do I need to send medical reports immediately?

No. Start with a short enquiry. We will explain how to share relevant documents and obtain your consent before they are sent to a hospital.
