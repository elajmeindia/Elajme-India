COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you send your case.

Which medical records should I share?

Start with a short medical summary, recent reports, relevant scans or imaging, and details of current or previous treatment. The hospital may request additional records after review.

Can my case be reviewed before I travel to India?

A remote hospital or specialist review may be possible depending on the case and provider. Elajme India can coordinate the request, but the medical team decides what can be assessed remotely.

Is a treatment estimate final?

No. A preliminary estimate may change after clinical evaluation, investigations, complications or changes in the recommended treatment.

Does Elajme India choose my treatment?

No. We coordinate information and communication. Diagnosis, treatment recommendations and clinical suitability are determined by qualified medical professionals.
