Sports medicine and sports injury care in India for international patients

Elajme India helps international patients coordinate sports injury consultations with orthopaedic and sports medicine teams in India. Specialists may review the injury, imaging, activity goals and rehabilitation history before advising on treatment and return-to-sport planning.

Sports injury assessment

Assessment may include the injury mechanism, activity level, pain, instability, examination findings, imaging and previous rehabilitation or treatment.

Orthopaedics & arthroscopy

For suitable musculoskeletal injuries, specialist review can determine whether rehabilitation, additional investigation, arthroscopy or another orthopaedic pathway should be considered.

Return to activity

The treating team determines the appropriate plan and timing for recovery and return to activity according to the individual diagnosis and clinical assessment.

Provider association, availability and suitability are reconfirmed for each case. Elajme India provides coordination and general information and does not diagnose, prescribe treatment or guarantee outcomes.

Records for review

Useful records can include injury details, X-ray, ultrasound or MRI reports and images, orthopaedic or physiotherapy notes, previous injections or surgery records, rehabilitation progress and current medicines.
