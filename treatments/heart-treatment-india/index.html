Heart-care enquiries we can coordinate

The appropriate care depends on the diagnosis, symptoms, investigations, previous treatment and overall health. A qualified cardiology or cardiac-surgery team must assess each case.

Cardiology review

Coordination for specialist review of heart conditions, investigations and previous treatment records.

Interventional cardiology

Selected cases may require specialist assessment for procedures such as angiography, angioplasty or stenting.

Cardiac surgery

Hospital review can help determine whether surgical assessment is appropriate for an individual case.

This page is for general information and coordination only. Elajme India does not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment or guarantee outcomes.

What to share for hospital review

Useful records may include a recent medical summary, ECG, echocardiogram, angiography or other cardiac reports and images if available, previous procedure records, current medicines and recent blood tests.
