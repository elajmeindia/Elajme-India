Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers about hospital enquiries in India, estimates, travel planning, language support and follow-up. If your question is not covered, you can contact our team.

Send an enquiry

Getting started

How do I start an enquiry?

Use the enquiry form or contact us on WhatsApp. Briefly describe the medical concern, preferred language and how we can reach you.

What medical information should I share?

A recent medical summary, relevant test reports and imaging reports can help a hospital understand the enquiry. Share only what is necessary through an agreed channel.

Will you share my reports without asking?

Medical reports may be shared with relevant hospitals or healthcare providers when reasonably necessary for the coordination you request. Ask us which provider will receive your information before sending reports, and review our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I speak with someone in Arabic?

You can contact Elajme India in Arabic or English. We help clarify the next coordination steps in your preferred language.

Does Elajme India provide medical advice?

No. We coordinate patient enquiries and practical arrangements. Qualified doctors and hospitals are responsible for diagnosis, clinical advice and treatment decisions.

Can I use the enquiry service for a medical emergency?

No. This website and its enquiry channels are not emergency medical services. If you have urgent or severe symptoms, contact local emergency services or seek immediate medical care.

Hospitals and estimates

Can you help me contact hospitals in India?

We can help prepare and forward an enquiry to relevant hospitals or specialist teams for their review. Acceptance and clinical recommendations depend on the provider.

Can you recommend a specific treatment?

Only qualified medical professionals can assess whether a treatment is suitable. We can help you seek an appropriate hospital review.

Can Elajme India guarantee treatment results or hospital acceptance?

No. Hospital acceptance, diagnosis, treatment recommendations and outcomes depend on the treating healthcare professionals and the patient's circumstances.

How much will treatment cost?

Costs depend on the hospital, condition, proposed treatment and clinical findings. Any estimate is preliminary until confirmed by the treating hospital.

Is a hospital estimate the final bill?

Not necessarily. Additional investigations, changes in treatment or length of stay may affect the final bill. Ask the hospital to explain inclusions and exclusions.

Will I pay Elajme India or the hospital?

Before making any payment, request written confirmation of the recipient, purpose, amount and applicable refund terms. Hospital charges and any coordination fees should be clearly distinguished. Do not send money to an unverified account.

Travel and stay

Can you help with medical visa documents?

We can help you understand which hospital documents to request and coordinate communication. Visa requirements and approval are determined by the relevant authorities.

Can you help arrange accommodation?

We can discuss accommodation and local support needs and help coordinate available options. Availability, prices and booking terms must be confirmed.

Is airport pickup available?

Please tell us your arrival city and dates. We can check available transport coordination options before you travel.

Can a family member accompany me?

Often patients travel with a companion, but visa eligibility, hospital rules and travel requirements must be checked for your circumstances.

Communication and follow-up

Will I have language support during my visit?

Tell us if you need Arabic or English communication support. We can discuss interpreter coordination and confirm availability for your location and dates.

How will I receive updates?

We can follow up through the agreed communication channel, such as WhatsApp or email, while clinical updates come from the treating hospital.

What happens after hospital discharge?

Follow the treating team's discharge instructions. We can help clarify practical follow-up arrangements and communication with the hospital where available.

How is my personal information handled?

We aim to request only information needed for your enquiry and share it with relevant providers as appropriate. Read our Privacy Policy before sending sensitive medical information.

Important: This information is general guidance, not medical advice or a guarantee of costs, visas or treatment outcomes. Clinical decisions belong to qualified healthcare professionals.
