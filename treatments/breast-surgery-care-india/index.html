Breast surgery and breast care in India for international patients

Elajme India coordinates breast-care enquiries with appropriate specialist teams. Diagnosis, investigations and treatment planning require individual clinical assessment.

What to share for review

Useful records can include mammography, ultrasound or breast MRI reports and images where available, biopsy/pathology results, previous breast-surgery or oncology notes, relevant blood tests and current medicines.

Elajme India provides international patient coordination and general information. We do not diagnose, prescribe treatment or guarantee acceptance or outcomes.
