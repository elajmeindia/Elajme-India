Understanding the treatment journey

Knee replacement may be considered for selected patients with significant knee damage, pain or reduced mobility. An orthopaedic specialist must determine whether surgery or another approach is appropriate.

Specialist review

The treating team reviews symptoms, medical history, imaging and previous treatment.

Surgical assessment

If surgery is considered, the orthopaedic team explains the proposed approach and patient-specific considerations.

Recovery planning

Rehabilitation and follow-up are important parts of recovery and vary according to the individual patient.

Elajme India provides coordination support only. Clinical recommendations and treatment decisions are made by qualified medical professionals.

What to share for hospital review

Useful documents may include a recent medical summary, knee X-rays or MRI reports and images if available, previous treatment or surgery records, current medicines and recent blood tests.
