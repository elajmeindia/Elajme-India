What to send for an Indian hospital review

A summary from your Ghanaian clinician

Share the latest referral, your diagnosis if known, the treatment already tried and the question you want the specialist in India to address.

Relevant imaging and lab results

Include dated reports relevant to the referral. Ask how to share original scans or large files and keep your own copies for the appointment.

Details for coordination

Tell us your city, preferred consultation language and whether a companion will travel. If you are submitting a relative’s case, confirm they have agreed to share their records.

India e-Medical Visa and travel checks from Ghana

Confirm your visa category

India’s official e-Visa portal lists Ghana as eligible. Check the e-Medical and e-Medical Attendant categories for your documents, the hospital letter and eligible arrival points. Ask the High Commission in Accra if your case requires another application route.

Check yellow fever requirements

India’s health authorities list Ghana among yellow fever risk countries. Check the certificate rules for your route with the airline and arrival health authority. If you have questions about vaccination eligibility, speak with a travel health clinician.

Confirm the city before booking

If departing from Accra, choose flights after the hospital confirms the appointment location. Ask the airline about connections, transit documents and any assistance needed for the patient.

Planning treatment in India from Ghana

Can I request a hospital estimate while I am in Ghana?

Yes. Send your medical summary through the enquiry channel, and we can request a preliminary estimate based on the records a hospital can review.

Can I apply for an e-Medical Visa with a Ghanaian passport?

Ghana is listed on the official e-Visa portal. Confirm your individual eligibility, the hospital letter and arrival requirements on the portal before booking.

Can someone travel with me from Ghana?

Check India’s separate Medical Attendant visa guidance and confirm which documents your companion needs. Include their travel costs when reviewing the budget.

Can I request a remote consultation first?

We can ask whether the specialist offers a remote appointment and confirm the fee, available times and any documents needed for review.

What do I need for follow-up care in Ghana?

Ask the Indian hospital for an English discharge summary, test results, medicines and follow-up instructions. Before returning, discuss who will receive the records and review your progress in Ghana.

What to confirm before booking from Ghana

Visa and attendant documents

On the e-Visa portal, verify passport eligibility, the official hospital letter, accepted port of entry and the accompanying person’s separate application. Check yellow fever documentation for departure and transit using the official health source above.

What does the estimate include?

Compare written costs in the same currency and confirm whether the deposit covers diagnostics, admission and procedure fees. When paying from Ghana, check conversion, bank fees and the hospital’s refund rules before transferring money.

Admission, recovery and follow-up

Ask the hospital to separate expected inpatient days from local recovery and review visits. Plan discharge documents, a current medicines list and a follow-up appointment with your clinician in Ghana before buying a fixed return ticket.
