Cardiology & cardiac care coordination

Heart treatment in India for international patients

Elajme India helps international patients and families coordinate cardiology and cardiac-surgery enquiries with hospitals and specialist teams in India, from medical-record review and consultation requests to practical treatment-visit planning.

Request coordination through Elajme India

Heart care depends on the individual diagnosis

The appropriate next step depends on symptoms, diagnosis, investigations, previous procedures, current medicines and overall health. A qualified cardiologist or cardiac surgeon must assess the individual case and recommend appropriate care.

Cardiology review

Specialist review may help assess heart conditions, symptoms, investigations and previous treatment records.

Interventional cardiology

Selected patients may be assessed for procedures such as coronary angiography, angioplasty or stenting when clinically appropriate.

Cardiac surgery

Some cases may require surgical assessment for procedures such as bypass or valve surgery after specialist evaluation.

This page provides general coordination information and is not medical advice. Elajme India does not diagnose heart conditions, prescribe treatment or guarantee outcomes.

What to share for preliminary review

Available records can help the hospital understand the case before a consultation. Useful documents may include a recent medical summary, ECG, echocardiogram, angiography reports or images when available, previous procedure records, current medicines and recent blood tests.

1. Share the case

Submit the main diagnosis or concern together with relevant available cardiac records.

2. Coordinate hospital review

Elajme India can route the enquiry to an appropriate hospital or specialist team for review.

3. Receive available medical feedback

Hospital feedback may include a consultation recommendation, additional investigations or an indicative treatment approach subject to clinical assessment.

4. Plan the treatment visit

If the patient chooses to proceed, we can coordinate appointments and practical hospital-visit requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Can Elajme India decide which heart procedure I need?

No. Procedure suitability and treatment decisions are made by qualified treating doctors after reviewing the patient's medical information and clinical condition.

Can I request a medical opinion before travelling?

Where a hospital or specialist offers remote case review or consultation, Elajme India can help coordinate the request. Availability depends on the medical team.

Is the treatment cost final before travel?

Any estimate provided before arrival is generally based on the information available at that time. Final investigations, treatment requirements and hospital charges may change after clinical assessment.

Discuss your heart-care enquiry

Share your available cardiac records with Elajme India. We can explain the coordination process and help route the enquiry for hospital review.
