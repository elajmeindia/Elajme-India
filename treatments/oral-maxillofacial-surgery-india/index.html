Preparing for specialist review

What the clinical team reviews

Assessment may involve the jaws, face, mouth, teeth or temporomandibular joint. Planning depends on the diagnosis, dental and facial imaging, bite, pathology when relevant and previous dental or facial procedures.

Records that can help

Send panoramic dental X-rays, CBCT or CT reports and images where available, dental notes, biopsy or pathology reports if relevant, previous surgery records and current medicines.

The treating specialist confirms investigations, suitability and treatment after individual review.
