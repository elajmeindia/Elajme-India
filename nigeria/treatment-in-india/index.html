Medical treatment in India for patients from Nigeria

Elajme India helps Nigerian patients and families coordinate treatment enquiries with hospitals and medical teams in India. We support the journey from initial case review and hospital communication to appointments and practical treatment-visit planning.

How Elajme India can support your treatment journey

Medical case coordination

We help organise relevant reports and coordinate your case with appropriate hospitals or specialist teams.

Hospital & appointment support

We coordinate hospital communication, available consultations, appointments and treatment estimates.

Travel & local coordination

If you decide to proceed, we can assist with practical arrangements connected with your treatment visit to India.

Documents that may help with case review

Depending on the condition, the hospital may request recent medical notes, laboratory results, scans or imaging reports, pathology results, previous treatment or surgery records and a current medication list. The medical team will confirm what is relevant to your case.

Planning the treatment visit

After the hospital reviews the case and advises the next steps, we can help coordinate appointments and practical treatment-visit arrangements. Visa and entry approvals are determined by the relevant authorities and are not guaranteed by Elajme India.

A clear first step

You can begin by sharing a short description of the medical need. Relevant reports can then be shared through the agreed communication process when needed for hospital coordination.

Medical note: Elajme India provides coordination support and does not make clinical diagnoses or guarantee outcomes. Medical recommendations must come from qualified doctors after reviewing the individual case.
