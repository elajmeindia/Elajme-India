ABOUT ELAJME INDIA

A coordination service for international patients.

Elajme India helps international patients navigate practical aspects of seeking healthcare in India, including communication with hospitals, case coordination and travel-related support.

Important

Elajme India provides coordination and general information only. Medical decisions are made by qualified healthcare professionals after appropriate clinical assessment.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you start your enquiry.

What should I send with my enquiry?

Share a brief description of the condition and relevant recent medical reports. Avoid sending unrelated personal documents.

Can Elajme India recommend a specific treatment?

No. Treatment recommendations and suitability decisions must come from qualified medical professionals after assessment.

Can you guarantee the treatment cost or outcome?

No. Costs may change after clinical evaluation, investigations or changes in treatment, and medical outcomes cannot be guaranteed.
