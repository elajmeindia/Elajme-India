# Elajme India — GCC Google Search Campaign Blueprints

Prepared for: UAE, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain

> Position Elajme India as an international patient coordination/facilitation service. Do not promise treatment outcomes, hospital acceptance, exact costs, visas or medical results. All enquiries must route through Elajme India.

## Measurement standard

Primary conversion: qualified patient enquiry through Elajme India.

Supporting events:
- `whatsapp_enquiry`
- `generate_lead`

Recommended UTM template:
`utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign={country}_{language}_{intent}&utm_content={adgroup}_{creative}&utm_term={keyword}`

Do not send patient names, phone numbers, diagnoses, reports or message text to analytics.

## Shared campaign architecture

For each country create separate Arabic and English Search campaigns. Keep brand/general medical-travel intent separate from treatment-specific intent so budget and search terms can be controlled independently.

Treatment groups:
1. Cancer care coordination
2. Heart/cardiac care coordination
3. Kidney transplant coordination
4. Liver transplant coordination
5. Knee replacement/orthopaedic coordination
6. Neurosurgery coordination
7. Spine surgery coordination
8. Urology coordination
9. Gastroenterology coordination

Use phrase/exact intent first. Expand only after search-term quality is proven.

Shared negative themes: jobs, vacancy, salary, course, degree, training, PDF, definition, free medicine, pharmacy, drug purchase, prescription purchase, wholesale, equipment, veterinary, DIY/home remedy, insurance-only research, and unrelated India tourism. Review actual search terms before adding negatives that could block genuine patient intent.

Avoid prescription-drug promotion, medicine sales, experimental/speculative treatment claims, guaranteed cure/success language, guaranteed visa language and misleading price claims.

## Launch controls

1. Confirm every final URL returns normally on mobile and desktop.
2. Confirm Arabic ads land on Arabic pages and English ads on English pages.
3. Test WhatsApp and enquiry CTAs and confirm they route only to Elajme India.
4. Verify UTM persistence and GA4 conversion events.
5. Do not upload medical reports or patient PII into Google Ads/GA4.
6. Review Google healthcare policy and local advertising rules again immediately before campaign activation.
7. Start with high-intent Search only and controlled budgets.
8. Review search terms frequently during the initial launch period.
9. Optimize toward qualified enquiries, not raw clicks or raw WhatsApp opens.

## Rollout order

Recommended operational rollout after Saudi Arabia:
1. UAE
2. Oman
3. Qatar
4. Kuwait
5. Bahrain

Do not activate all markets at full scale simultaneously. Validate search-term quality, qualified-lead rate and follow-up capacity before increasing spend.
