Medical visa and travel from the Republic of the Congo

Check the correct visa route

The Indian Embassy in Brazzaville lists medical reports translated into English, an admission letter from the Indian hospital and proof of funds for stay and treatment. Use its checklist for the Medical and Medical Attendant applications.

Yellow fever and transit

The Embassy in Brazzaville advises travellers from the Republic of the Congo to carry yellow fever documentation. Check current entry and transit requirements for the patient and attendant. Discuss individual vaccine eligibility with a travel health clinician.

Book around the confirmed appointment

Confirm whether your appointment is at the main hospital or another campus. Verify available routes from Brazzaville or Pointe-Noire with your carrier and check transit documents. Choose accommodation and local transport based on mobility needs and travel time to the hospital.

Use the official Indian regular visa portal and confirm submission procedures and required documents for the Medical Visa and Medical Attendant Visa with the embassy, according to your passport.

Questions before travelling from Brazzaville

Which embassy handles my medical visa?

Use the Visa Services guidance from the Indian Embassy in Brazzaville and verify the route for your passport. Ask the mission about the patient and attendant documents individually.

Can the hospital review a report written in French?

Keep the original and ask which reports must be translated into English. Check whether the hospital can arrange an interpreter and the fee before confirming a visit.

Can I review the cost before I pay a deposit?

Ask for a written estimate with inclusions, exclusions and deposit refund terms. Compare the same services across hospitals and verify what will change if more tests are needed.

Can I request an appointment before booking flights?

We can ask the department to review your referral and propose a date. Confirm the address, expected tests and what needs to be brought for admission.

How long should a companion plan to stay?

Ask about expected admission, local recovery and follow-up. Check accommodation and banking arrangements and allow for a change of return date.

What paperwork will I need back in the Republic of the Congo?

Request a discharge summary, procedure details, relevant test results, medicines and a review schedule. Confirm how to share these with your local doctor and whether translation will be required.
