Wound care and hyperbaric medicine in India for international patients

Elajme India helps international families coordinate wound care & hyperbaric medicine enquiries with appropriate specialist teams in India. Diagnosis, investigations and treatment planning require individual specialist assessment.

Care depends on individual assessment

Specialist assessment

The specialist team reviews symptoms, diagnosis, previous treatment, relevant investigations and medical history for complex wound and hyperbaric medicine needs.

Condition-specific care

Depending on the condition, additional investigations or multidisciplinary specialist review may be recommended by the treating team.

Treatment and follow-up

The treating team determines appropriate investigations, treatment options and follow-up according to the individual clinical assessment.

Elajme India provides international patient coordination and general information. We do not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment or guarantee treatment acceptance or outcomes.

Wound and hyperbaric review

Specialist review considers the wound type and duration, signs of infection, blood supply, diabetes or pressure-related risk, previous surgery and wound treatments. Hyperbaric oxygen therapy is considered only when the treating team identifies an appropriate clinical indication.

What the team reviews

Wound location and duration, infection concerns, circulation, diabetes, pressure risk, previous operations, current dressings and response to earlier treatment.

Useful records

Recent wound photographs, wound and surgery notes, culture results, vascular studies, diabetes records, relevant imaging, current dressings or treatments and medicine list.

Hyperbaric treatment

Hyperbaric oxygen is not appropriate for every wound. Eligibility, expected benefit, contraindications and the treatment plan must be determined by the treating medical team after assessment.
