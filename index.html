FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions international patients often ask.

How do I start a treatment enquiry?

You can contact us on WhatsApp or complete the enquiry form on this page. Share your basic details and treatment requirement, and we will guide you on the next steps.

Do you provide medical advice?

No. Elajme India provides healthcare facilitation and coordination services. Medical advice, diagnosis and treatment decisions are provided by qualified doctors and hospitals.

Can you help me with the medical visa process?

We can help explain the practical steps and coordinate supporting hospital documentation where available. Visa applications and approvals remain subject to the relevant Indian authorities.

When should I share my medical reports?

After your initial enquiry, we will guide you on which relevant reports are needed. You can then share them directly in the WhatsApp conversation for coordination with the hospital or medical team.

Can you guarantee treatment results or costs?

No. Treatment outcomes cannot be guaranteed, and costs may change depending on the patient's condition, medical findings, hospital policies and the treatment ultimately required.
