Dental care coordination

Dental treatment in India for international patients

Elajme India helps international patients coordinate dental-care enquiries with qualified dental teams in India. The appropriate procedure, investigations and treatment plan depend on individual clinical assessment.

Request coordination through Elajme India

Dental care depends on individual assessment

Dental consultation

A dental specialist may review symptoms, dental history, available X-rays or scans and previous treatment before advising next steps.

Restorative and surgical care

Depending on the case, the treating team may discuss restorative, prosthodontic, implant-related, oral-surgical or other dental options after evaluation.

Further investigations

The treating team may request additional dental imaging or tests before confirming suitability, treatment stages, timing or estimates.

Elajme India provides coordination and general information. We do not diagnose dental conditions, prescribe procedures or guarantee treatment outcomes.

What to share for an initial review

Useful information may include the main dental concern, previous dental records, available X-rays or scans, relevant medical history and current medicines. The treating dental team determines what records and investigations are required.

1. Send available records

Share the main concern and relevant available dental or medical documents with Elajme India.

2. Coordinate specialist review

We can coordinate submission to an appropriate dental team for preliminary review.

3. Confirm next steps

Consultation availability, investigations, procedure options, timing and estimates remain subject to the treating provider's review.
