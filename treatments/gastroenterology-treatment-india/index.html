How we can support you

Medical report coordination

We help organise relevant medical information for hospital review.

Specialist coordination

We coordinate communication, consultations, appointments and available hospital estimates.

Travel support

If you proceed with treatment, we can assist with practical visit coordination.

Preparing for specialist review

The information needed varies by condition. A specialist may ask for consultation notes, laboratory results, imaging, endoscopy reports, pathology results, previous treatment records or a medication list. The hospital will advise what is relevant for the individual case.
