ENT care coordination

ENT treatment in India for international patients

Elajme India helps international patients coordinate enquiries for ear, nose and throat conditions with appropriate ENT teams in India. Diagnosis, investigations and treatment depend on individual specialist assessment.

Request coordination through Elajme India

ENT care depends on the condition

Ear and hearing concerns

ENT assessment may be relevant for persistent ear symptoms, hearing concerns and other ear-related conditions.

Nose and sinus conditions

Patients with chronic nasal or sinus symptoms may require specialist examination and diagnosis-specific investigations.

Throat, voice and related concerns

Persistent throat, swallowing or voice concerns can require ENT evaluation and coordination with other specialties where appropriate.

Elajme India provides international patient coordination and general information. We do not diagnose ENT conditions, prescribe treatment or guarantee outcomes.

What to share for an initial review

Useful information may include the diagnosis or main concern, recent ENT consultation notes, hearing or audiology reports, endoscopy findings, scans or imaging, pathology reports where relevant, previous procedures and current medicines.

1. Send available records

Share the main ENT concern and relevant available medical records with Elajme India.

2. Coordinate specialist review

We can coordinate submission to an appropriate ENT hospital or specialist team for preliminary review.

3. Confirm next steps

Consultation availability, investigations, treatment options, timing and estimates remain subject to the treating provider’s review.

Preparing for an ENT specialist review

What the clinical team reviews

ENT assessment varies for ear, hearing, nose, sinus, throat, voice and head-and-neck concerns. The specialist may need endoscopy, hearing tests or imaging depending on the symptoms and suspected diagnosis.

Records that can help

Send recent ENT notes, hearing tests or audiograms, endoscopy findings, CT or MRI reports and images where available, pathology reports when relevant, previous ENT surgery records and current medicines.

The treating team confirms diagnosis, suitability, investigations and treatment after individual review. Elajme India coordinates case submission and communication and does not replace medical advice.
