Understanding knee replacement

Knee replacement may be considered for some people with severe knee damage, pain and reduced mobility when other treatments have not provided sufficient relief. An orthopaedic specialist must assess the individual case.

Specialist review

The orthopaedic team reviews symptoms, medical history, imaging and previous treatment.

Surgical planning

If surgery is recommended, the treating team explains the proposed procedure and individual considerations.

Recovery planning

Rehabilitation and follow-up are important parts of recovery and vary by patient.

This page provides general coordination information and is not medical advice. Diagnosis and treatment decisions are made by qualified medical professionals.

What to share for hospital review

Relevant documents may include a recent medical summary, knee X-rays or MRI reports and images if available, previous treatment or surgery records, current medicines and recent blood-test reports.
