Before you contact us

You can initially share the patient's country, age, medical condition or diagnosis if known, treatment required if known, and a brief summary of the current situation.

Relevant medical reports can be shared through an agreed communication channel when needed for case coordination. Please avoid posting sensitive medical information publicly.

What happens next?

We review the coordination request, clarify any missing information, and where appropriate coordinate the case with a hospital or specialist team in India. Medical recommendations are made by qualified medical professionals after reviewing the case.

Important: For emergencies or urgent medical deterioration, contact local emergency medical services rather than waiting for an online coordination response.
