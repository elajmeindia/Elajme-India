Kidney transplant coordination in India

Elajme India helps international patients coordinate kidney transplant enquiries with transplant hospitals and specialist teams in India. Eligibility, donor evaluation and required approvals depend on individual medical assessment and applicable legal and hospital requirements.

How we support the coordination process

Patient & donor information

We help organise relevant records requested by the transplant hospital for preliminary review.

Hospital communication

We coordinate responses, consultations and next-step information from the transplant team.

Treatment-visit planning

We support practical arrangements after the hospital advises how the case may proceed.

Before planning travel

Transplant cases can require medical, donor and documentation review before travel is appropriate. The hospital or relevant authority may request additional information or approvals. Patients should wait for the transplant team’s case-specific guidance before making treatment-related travel decisions.

Important: Elajme India does not determine transplant eligibility, donor suitability or legal approval and does not guarantee acceptance or outcomes. These decisions are made by qualified medical teams and relevant authorities under applicable requirements.
