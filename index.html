FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to your questions about treatment in India

What medical reports should you share?

Start with a brief summary of your condition, any known diagnosis, recent test results, relevant scans and information about previous treatment. The hospital may request additional records.

Can your medical case be reviewed before travelling to India?

A review of medical reports or a remote consultation may be available, depending on the hospital, specialty and your condition. The treating medical team determines what can be assessed remotely.

How can you find a suitable hospital or doctor in India?

We help you communicate with relevant hospitals and medical teams and share available information to support your comparison. You choose your healthcare provider in consultation with qualified clinicians.

Who decides which treatment is right for you?

Qualified doctors are responsible for diagnosis, treatment recommendations and clinical suitability. Elajme India coordinates communication and practical arrangements, not medical decisions.

How can you obtain an estimated treatment cost?

Where available, we can help request a preliminary estimate from the hospital after the relevant medical information has been reviewed. Final costs may change following examinations, tests or changes to the treatment plan.

Can Elajme India help you arrange hospital appointments?

We can help coordinate requests for online or in-person consultations and hospital appointments, subject to provider availability and confirmation.

Can Elajme India help with medical visa documentation?

We can help coordinate relevant information and documentation when needed. Visa eligibility, requirements and approval are determined by the appropriate authorities and cannot be guaranteed.

Can Elajme India help with travel and accommodation?

We can assist with information and coordination for accommodation, airport transfers and other practical arrangements where available. Any arrangements and costs should be confirmed before booking.

Can you communicate with Elajme India in Arabic or English?

Yes. You can contact our team in Arabic or English to discuss your enquiry and follow the coordination process.

Are hospital acceptance or treatment outcomes guaranteed?

No. Hospital acceptance, treatment recommendations and outcomes depend on individual clinical assessment and decisions by healthcare providers. We explain available information but cannot guarantee medical results.
