Liver transplant in India for international patients

Elajme India helps international patients and families coordinate liver-transplant enquiries with transplant hospitals and specialist teams in India. Medical suitability, donor evaluation, documentation and approvals are determined by qualified transplant teams and the applicable hospital and legal processes.

Request liver transplant coordination

Understanding the transplant evaluation process

A liver transplant is a complex medical and regulatory process. The transplant team must review the patient’s diagnosis, severity of liver disease, overall health, previous treatment and available donor information before advising whether and how evaluation can proceed.

Patient evaluation

The hospital may review liver-disease history, recent medical summaries, laboratory results, imaging, previous treatment and other investigations relevant to transplant assessment.

Potential donor evaluation

For living-donor cases, the potential donor requires a separate medical assessment and documentation according to the transplant programme and applicable requirements.

Hospital and regulatory process

Transplant cases may require hospital committee review and other approvals. Requirements vary by individual case and must be confirmed by the treating hospital and relevant authorities.

Important: Elajme India is a patient-coordination service. We do not determine transplant eligibility, donor suitability or legal approval, and we do not guarantee hospital acceptance, transplantation or treatment outcomes.

What to share for preliminary review

Providing organised and recent records can help the transplant team understand the case before advising the next steps.

Patient medical records

Recent medical summary, diagnosis, liver-function and other laboratory reports, imaging reports and relevant previous treatment records.

Potential donor information

Basic donor information and available medical records can be shared when requested. The hospital decides which formal tests and documents are required.

Previous consultations

Include previous transplant recommendations, discharge summaries and information about current medicines when available.

How Elajme India coordinates your enquiry

1. Share the case

Submit the patient’s available reports and, where relevant, basic potential-donor information through Elajme India.

2. Transplant-team review

We coordinate the enquiry with an appropriate hospital for preliminary specialist review and case-specific requirements.

3. Consultation and documentation

We help coordinate consultations and communication about additional medical or administrative documents requested by the hospital.

4. Treatment-visit planning

When the hospital advises that evaluation can proceed, we can assist with appointment and practical treatment-visit coordination.

Frequently asked questions

Can Elajme India confirm whether I am eligible for a liver transplant?

No. Eligibility can only be determined by a qualified transplant team after reviewing the individual medical case and completing the required evaluation.

Can a potential donor be approved before travelling?

A hospital may review preliminary information before travel, but formal donor suitability and required approvals depend on the transplant programme, medical testing and applicable requirements.

Can you provide the final treatment cost?

We can coordinate available hospital estimates after case review. Final costs may change according to evaluation findings, treatment requirements, hospital stay and other clinical factors.
