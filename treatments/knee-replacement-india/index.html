Understanding knee replacement assessment

Knee replacement may be considered for selected patients with significant joint damage, persistent pain or reduced mobility. The decision depends on symptoms, examination, imaging, previous treatment, general health and an orthopaedic specialist's assessment.

Specialist review

The orthopaedic team reviews symptoms, medical history, X-rays or other imaging and treatments already tried.

Surgical assessment

If surgery is considered, the treating team determines the appropriate procedure and explains patient-specific benefits, limitations and risks.

Pre-operative planning

Additional investigations and medical clearance may be requested before surgery depending on the patient's health and hospital protocol.

Rehabilitation

Physiotherapy, mobility exercises and follow-up form an important part of recovery, with the programme tailored by the treating team.

This page provides general coordination information and is not medical advice. Elajme India does not determine whether knee replacement is appropriate and does not guarantee surgical or rehabilitation outcomes.

What to share for preliminary review

Useful information may include a recent medical summary, knee X-rays or MRI reports and images when available, previous surgery or treatment records, current medicines, relevant health conditions and recent laboratory reports.

Frequently asked questions

Does every patient with knee pain need replacement surgery?

No. The appropriate treatment depends on the cause and severity of symptoms, imaging findings, previous treatment and specialist assessment.

Can my reports be reviewed before travelling?

Available records and imaging can be shared for preliminary hospital review. The hospital may request additional investigations or an in-person examination.

How long should I stay in India?

The required stay varies by procedure, recovery and the treating team's advice. Travel plans should be based on case-specific hospital guidance.

Planning knee replacement care in India

How the case is assessed

Knee replacement is considered after an orthopaedic assessment of pain, mobility, joint damage and response to non-surgical treatment. The surgeon determines whether partial or total replacement is appropriate after reviewing the patient and imaging.

Records to prepare for review

Send recent standing knee X-rays or other available imaging, orthopaedic notes, details of previous injections or surgery, current medicines and relevant medical conditions.

Treatment recommendations, timelines and suitability can only be confirmed by the treating clinical team after reviewing the individual case. Elajme India coordinates the enquiry and case-review process and does not replace medical advice.
