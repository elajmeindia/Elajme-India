Neurosurgery in India for international patients

Elajme India coordinates neurological and neurosurgical enquiries with hospitals and specialist teams in India, helping international patients organise records, request specialist review and plan the practical steps of a treatment visit.

Cases that may require specialist review

Neurological and neurosurgical conditions vary widely. Depending on the diagnosis, patients may need assessment by a neurologist, neurosurgeon, neuro-oncology team or another specialist. The appropriate pathway can only be determined after individual medical review.

Brain and cranial conditions

Specialist review may be requested for selected brain tumours, vascular conditions, hydrocephalus and other cranial disorders.

Spine and nerve conditions

Some patients with spinal or nerve-related conditions may need neurological or neurosurgical assessment alongside spine specialists.

Complex second opinions

Patients with an existing diagnosis or proposed surgery can share available records for a hospital team to review and advise on possible next steps.

Important: Elajme India provides coordination support and does not diagnose neurological conditions, recommend surgery, prescribe treatment or guarantee outcomes.

What to share for specialist review

Where available, share a recent medical summary, consultation notes, MRI or CT reports and image files, pathology or biopsy reports when relevant, previous surgery or treatment records, current medicines and recent laboratory results. The receiving hospital may request additional tests.

Frequently asked questions

Does every neurological condition require surgery?

No. Treatment depends on the diagnosis and individual circumstances. A qualified specialist must determine whether surgery, medical treatment, monitoring or another approach is appropriate.

Can imaging be reviewed before travelling?

Hospitals may review available reports and imaging before travel, subject to the quality and completeness of the records and the hospital's review process.

Can Elajme India arrange an online consultation?

Where a hospital or specialist offers remote consultation, Elajme India can help coordinate the request and available scheduling.

Are treatment costs final before arrival?

Any preliminary estimate is subject to specialist assessment, investigations, treatment decisions and hospital policy. Final charges can change according to the actual care required.
