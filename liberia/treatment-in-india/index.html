Organise your referral and test results in Liberia

Explain the referral

Ask your clinician for a summary stating the working diagnosis, past treatment, current medicines and the decision you want the Indian team to review.

Include the original results

Provide readable reports with dates and units. If you only have photos of results or a disc with scans, ask which items can be reviewed remotely and how to send the originals.

Share through an agreed channel

Start with the enquiry form or ask on WhatsApp how to send files. Obtain permission before submitting records for a relative and keep originals for admission.

Start a medical enquiry

Compare care plans and costs before leaving Liberia

Choose a department

Match the referral to a hospital department. Ask which clinician will review the case, where tests are carried out and whether a remote consultation is available.

Ask for a written plan

Request likely evaluation steps, the proposed procedure if one is being considered, and the documents the hospital will need before scheduling admission.

Check charges and deposits

Compare like-for-like quotes. Ask about diagnostics, consultant fees, operating or procedure charges, medicines, inpatient days, deposits, ICU exclusions and refunds for rescheduling.

Prepare for discharge

Ask how long to remain near the hospital and whether more than one review visit is planned. Arrange to take a discharge summary and the treatment and medicine plan back to the clinician in Liberia.

Keep hospital bills in their quoted currency and build a separate budget for flights, visa fees, meals, transfers and accommodation. Confirm with your bank whether an international card or transfer can cover the deposit from Liberia. Only the hospital can confirm charges after assessment.

Request a written hospital estimate

Which hospital department should review your case?

For an oncology referral, include pathology and previous treatment details. For heart care, include any relevant cardiac testing. For a neurological or orthopaedic problem, ask the hospital what images it needs and whether the evaluation and any rehabilitation are available at the proposed campus.

Medical visa and travel from Liberia to India

Choose the visa route

Liberia appears on India’s e-Visa eligibility list. Confirm personal eligibility and check the e-Medical and corresponding e-Medical Attendant categories separately. The Embassy in Monrovia publishes regular-visa instructions, including hospital invitation requirements. Its medical checklist also lists a referral from a local doctor or hospital and an applicant bank statement.

Check passport and health documentation

Confirm your passport scan, photo, hospital letter and any companion documents on the official portal. Check Indian entry rules for yellow fever documentation based on departure and transit. Ask the airline and an appropriate travel health clinician about what applies to the patient and companion.

Plan connections and accommodation

Confirm the appointment campus before choosing flights from the Monrovia area. Verify transit permissions and connection arrangements with the airline. Choose accommodation by actual travel time to the hospital and confirm transfers and accessibility if needed.

The e-Medical application checklist includes a passport scan and a letter from the Indian hospital with a proposed admission date. Verify all current uploads, English-language document requirements and the separate attendant category on the portal.

FAQs for patients travelling from Liberia

Can I get a second opinion while I am in Liberia?

We can ask the hospital whether it can arrange a remote review, what evidence the specialist needs and whether a fee applies.

Can the estimate be issued before the trip?

An Indian hospital may provide preliminary costs after reviewing records. Ask which tests, room charges and medicines are included and what might change after evaluation.

What if I do not have electronic scans?

Tell us what you have. The hospital can specify which written reports it can use and whether diagnostic image files are required.

What documents does my companion need?

Check the Medical Attendant category on the official portal. Confirm with the hospital whether companion details belong in the patient’s letter and whether any relationship or other evidence is required.

How much time should I reserve for the trip?

Ask the hospital for the expected time for diagnostics, admission, local recovery and rechecks. Confirm with the clinical team when it can assess whether it is appropriate to fly home.

Can I continue follow-up in Liberia?

Before discharge, ask which visits can be performed locally, what written instructions your clinician needs and whether the treating hospital can arrange remote follow-up.
