Transplant coordination

Kidney transplant coordination in India

Elajme India coordinates international patient kidney-transplant enquiries with transplant hospitals and specialist teams in India. Eligibility, donor evaluation and approvals depend on individual medical assessment and applicable legal and hospital requirements.

Request coordination through Elajme India

How we support the coordination process

Patient and donor records

We help organise relevant records requested by the transplant hospital for preliminary review.

Transplant-team communication

We coordinate consultation requests and case-specific next-step information from the hospital team.

Treatment-visit planning

Practical arrangements can be coordinated after the hospital advises how the case may proceed.

Important: Elajme India does not determine transplant eligibility, donor suitability or legal approval and does not guarantee acceptance or outcomes. These decisions are made by qualified medical teams and relevant authorities.
