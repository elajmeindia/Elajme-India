TRANSPLANT COORDINATION

Liver transplant coordination in India

Elajme India supports international patients seeking coordination with liver transplant hospitals and specialist teams in India. Medical suitability, donor evaluation and required approvals are determined through specialist assessment and applicable legal and hospital processes.

Treatment visit

Case review comes before travel planning

A transplant team may require detailed medical and donor information before advising whether further evaluation in India is appropriate.

Important: Elajme India does not decide transplant eligibility or donor suitability and does not guarantee approval, acceptance or treatment outcomes.
