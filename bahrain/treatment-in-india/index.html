Medical treatment in India for patients from Bahrain

Elajme India helps patients and families from Bahrain coordinate medical-case reviews with hospitals and specialists in India, consultation requests, available treatment estimates and practical planning for a treatment visit. Arabic and English communication support is available throughout the coordination journey.

Request Case Review

Before you plan travel to India

Whenever possible, start with a remote review of the available medical records. This can help the receiving hospital understand the case and advise what additional information, consultation or investigation may be required before travel.

Medical summary

Share the diagnosis or main medical concern, current symptoms, important medical history and previous treatment.

Reports and scans

Include recent blood tests, imaging reports and images, pathology or biopsy reports, procedure notes and discharge summaries where relevant.

Current medicines

Provide a current medication list and mention significant allergies or ongoing treatment when known.

Questions and priorities

Tell us what you want reviewed, such as a second opinion, treatment options, consultation, expected stay or an available hospital estimate.

How Elajme India coordinates your case

1. Share your case

Submit the medical concern and available records through Elajme India.

2. Case routing

We coordinate the enquiry with an appropriate hospital or specialist team based on the information provided.

3. Review available feedback

We relay available medical feedback, requested investigations, consultation information and hospital estimates. Final recommendations depend on specialist assessment.

4. Coordinate the visit

If you choose to proceed, we can assist with appointment coordination and practical treatment-related travel, stay and local arrangements when required.

Remote consultation and treatment estimates

A hospital may be able to review reports remotely or arrange an online consultation before travel, depending on the case and specialist availability. Where an estimate is provided, it should be treated as preliminary: the final treatment plan and cost may change after examination, additional tests, procedure requirements, length of stay or clinical findings in India.

Elajme India coordinates communication and patient support. We do not diagnose, prescribe treatment, select a procedure on behalf of a doctor, or guarantee hospital acceptance, treatment results, final costs or visa approval.

Support for patients and accompanying family

Arabic and English coordination

We support communication during the coordination process so patients and families can understand the available information and next steps.

Appointments

We can coordinate available hospital and specialist appointments after the case has been reviewed.

Travel planning

When required, we can assist with treatment-related travel planning, accommodation and local transfer coordination. Visa decisions remain with the relevant authorities.

Follow-up coordination

After treatment, we can help coordinate available hospital follow-up communication and document exchange.

Frequently asked questions

Can my reports be reviewed before I travel?

Often, yes. Whether a remote review is possible and which documents are required depends on the hospital, specialist and medical condition.

Can I request an online consultation?

We can coordinate the request. Availability and suitability are determined by the hospital or specialist.

Will I know the treatment cost before travelling?

A hospital may provide a preliminary estimate after reviewing sufficient records. It is not a guaranteed final bill and may change after clinical evaluation.

Does Elajme India arrange visas?

We can provide treatment-related coordination and available supporting information, but visa eligibility and approval are decided by the relevant authorities.

Urgent medical symptoms: if the patient has severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, major bleeding, loss of consciousness, stroke-like symptoms or sudden serious deterioration, seek emergency medical care locally rather than waiting for website coordination.
