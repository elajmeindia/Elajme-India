Prepare medical records from Mauritania

Local medical advice

Obtain a dated referral or assessment that outlines your medical history, treatments and why your clinician wants a specialist opinion in India.

Arabic and French reports

Keep the original reports. Ask the hospital which files need English translation and whether it requires a particular format. Ask about French interpretation with the treating hospital if needed.

Relevant results and images

Include tests and scans that relate to the referral. Ask how to send large files, and keep copies of the original report, translated version and hospital response together.

Consent and first contact

Start with the enquiry form or ask on WhatsApp which records to send. If you are arranging care for another adult, obtain their permission before sharing the case.

India medical visa from Mauritania

Use the official e-Medical route if eligible

The Embassy of India in Nouakchott says holders of ordinary Mauritanian passports can apply electronically for medical treatment and accompanying attendant categories. Check passport, photo, hospital invitation and permitted arrival details on Indian Visa Online.

Confirm the regular visa checklist if needed

The Nouakchott mission lists preliminary advice from a local doctor or hospital as part of its regular medical visa guidance. Confirm the required Indian hospital documents, translations, attendant application and any supporting financial evidence directly with the mission.

Confirm the hospital city and route

Check travel options from Nouakchott against your confirmed appointment. Ask about airport connections and transit documents, yellow fever rules for your itinerary, baggage arrangements and mobility support. Plan accommodation and transfers around the hospital location.

The e-Medical application checklist includes a passport scan and a letter from the Indian hospital with a proposed admission date. Verify all current uploads, English-language document requirements and the separate attendant category on the portal.

Practical questions from Mauritanian patients

Can I ask for a specialist opinion from Nouakchott?

We can request review of the available records or ask about a remote appointment. The hospital decides what can be reviewed remotely and what needs examination in person.

Do French reports need translating before I contact you?

Keep the French originals and tell us what you have. Ask which documents the hospital or visa authority needs in English before commissioning a translation.

Can I apply online for a medical visa with a Mauritanian passport?

The Indian Embassy in Nouakchott lists the service for ordinary Mauritanian passports. Check the current eligibility, upload checklist and conditions on the official portal.

What if a family member will accompany me?

Check the accompanying medical attendant category and required supporting documents. Ask the hospital how to document the companion when requesting the hospital letter.

How do I compare prices for a proposed procedure?

Ask for the same scope across estimates and check which hospital days, tests, medicines, rehabilitation and additional care are excluded. Review your travel and accommodation budget separately.

How much time should I allow before returning?

Ask for separate estimates for testing, inpatient care, local recovery and follow-up. Ask when the treating team can assess fitness for air travel.

What should I take back for follow-up?

Request a discharge summary, treatment and medicines list, copies of results and a follow-up schedule. Confirm the preferred language for handing these records back to your doctor in Mauritania.
