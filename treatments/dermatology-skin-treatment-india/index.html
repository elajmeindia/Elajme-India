Dermatology care coordination

Dermatology and skin treatment in India for international patients

If you are seeking dermatology or skin treatment in India, Elajme India can help coordinate an enquiry with qualified dermatologists. We assist with sharing relevant reports and requesting specialist review for skin, hair or nail concerns.

Request coordination through Elajme India

Skin, hair and nail conditions requiring dermatology review

Chronic and inflammatory skin conditions

Persistent rashes, eczema, psoriasis and related conditions may require dermatology assessment and diagnosis-specific management.

Hair, scalp and nail concerns

Specialist review can help determine appropriate investigations and management for hair loss, scalp disorders and nail conditions.

Skin lesions and procedural dermatology

Suspicious or persistent lesions may require clinical examination, dermoscopy, biopsy or another procedure as determined by the treating dermatologist.

Elajme India provides international patient coordination and general information. We do not diagnose skin conditions, prescribe treatment or guarantee outcomes.

What to share for an initial review

Useful information may include the diagnosis or main concern, dermatology notes, clear recent photographs where appropriate, pathology or biopsy reports, relevant blood tests, previous treatments, allergies and current medicines.

1. Send available records

Share the main concern and relevant available records with Elajme India.

2. Coordinate specialist review

We can coordinate submission to an appropriate dermatology team for preliminary review.

3. Confirm next steps

Consultation, investigations, treatment options, procedures, timing and estimates remain subject to the treating provider’s review.

Preparing for a dermatology review

What the clinical team reviews

Skin conditions can be inflammatory, infectious, autoimmune, pigment-related or tumour-related. A useful review depends on the appearance and distribution of the condition, duration, previous diagnoses, medicines and any biopsy or laboratory findings.

Records that can help

Send clear recent photographs of the affected area where appropriate, dermatology notes, biopsy or laboratory reports, allergy or medication history, previous treatments and a list of current medicines or topical products.

The treating team confirms diagnosis, suitability, investigations and treatment after individual review. Elajme India coordinates case submission and communication and does not replace medical advice.
