UAE → India patient coordination

Medical treatment in India for patients from the UAE

Elajme India helps patients and families in the United Arab Emirates coordinate medical-case review with hospitals and specialists in India, consultation requests, available treatment estimates and practical treatment-visit planning. Arabic and English communication support is available.

Request Case Review

What to share before planning travel

Medical summary

Share the diagnosis or main medical concern, symptoms, previous treatment and current medicines where relevant.

Reports and imaging

Include recent laboratory reports, pathology, discharge summaries and CT, MRI, X-ray or other imaging reports when available.

Previous treatment records

For complex or ongoing cases, prior surgery, chemotherapy, procedure or specialist records can help the receiving team understand the history.

Patient details

Age, country of residence and any important medical conditions can help us route the enquiry appropriately.

Your UAE-to-India coordination journey

1. Share your case

Send the available medical information through Elajme India.

2. Hospital review

We coordinate the enquiry with an appropriate hospital or specialist team in India.

3. Review available feedback

We share available consultation information, proposed next steps and hospital estimates where provided.

4. Plan the treatment visit

If you choose to proceed, we help coordinate appointments and practical treatment-related arrangements.

Elajme India coordinates the process but does not diagnose, prescribe treatment or decide clinical suitability. Medical decisions remain with qualified treating professionals.

Support during treatment planning

Arabic & English communication

We help reduce communication gaps while coordinating with hospitals and medical teams.

Remote consultation requests

Where a hospital or specialist offers remote consultation, Elajme India can help coordinate the request before travel.

Hospital estimates

We can request available preliminary estimates. Final charges may change after examination, investigations, procedure choice, implant or medicine requirements and length of stay.

Travel and local coordination

When required, we can assist with treatment-related appointment planning, accommodation, airport transfer and other practical coordination.

Frequently asked questions

Can I send reports before travelling from the UAE?

Yes. Available records can be coordinated for preliminary hospital or specialist review before you make a treatment-travel decision.

Can Elajme India arrange an online consultation?

We can coordinate a remote consultation request where the selected hospital or specialist offers that service.

Is a hospital estimate the final treatment cost?

No. Preliminary estimates can change after clinical assessment, additional tests, procedure selection, medicines, implants, complications or changes in hospital stay.

Do you guarantee a medical visa or hospital acceptance?

No. Hospital acceptance depends on case review, and visa decisions are made by the relevant authorities. Elajme India can only support coordination and available documentation.

For urgent or life-threatening symptoms, seek immediate emergency medical care where you are. This website enquiry service is not an emergency service.

Planning treatment in India from the UAE?

Share the medical concern and available reports with Elajme India. We will help coordinate the next appropriate step with a hospital or specialist team.
