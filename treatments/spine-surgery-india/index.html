SPINE CARE

Spine surgery treatment coordination in India

Elajme India helps international patients coordinate spine-related case reviews with hospitals and specialist teams in India. The appropriate investigation or treatment depends on the individual diagnosis, symptoms, imaging and specialist assessment.

How we support your enquiry

Medical case review

We help organise relevant reports and imaging information for specialist review.

Hospital coordination

We coordinate hospital communication, consultations, appointments and available estimates.

Treatment-visit planning

If you decide to proceed, we assist with practical arrangements connected with your visit to India.

Information that may help with preliminary review

Depending on the condition, a specialist may request recent consultation notes, MRI or CT reports and images, previous surgery records and a current medication list. The medical team will advise what is relevant for the individual case.
