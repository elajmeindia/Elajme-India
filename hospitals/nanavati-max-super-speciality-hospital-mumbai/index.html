International patient coordination

Nanavati Max is part of the Max Healthcare hospital network. Max Healthcare lists international-patient support including medical-report review and appointment assistance, medical-visa invitation documentation, interpreter support, transportation and accommodation assistance. Availability and the exact support applicable to an individual case must be reconfirmed before travel.

Elajme India can coordinate preliminary medical-report submission, hospital communication and specialist consultation requests for patients considering care at Nanavati Max.
