Medical treatment in India for patients from Saudi Arabia

How the coordination process works

1. Share your enquiry

Tell us the treatment or specialty you are looking for and provide relevant medical information when requested.

2. Hospital review

We coordinate your case with relevant hospitals or specialist teams for review and available treatment information.

3. Plan the next steps

If you choose to proceed, we assist with appointments and practical treatment-visit coordination.

Preparing your medical information

Useful records may include recent consultation notes, investigation reports, scans or imaging reports, pathology results where relevant, previous treatment or surgery records and a current medication list. The hospital will advise which documents are required for your individual case.

Travel and treatment planning

Once a hospital has reviewed the case, we can help coordinate the next practical steps connected with the proposed treatment visit. Any medical visa or entry requirements remain subject to the applicable authorities and current rules; Elajme India does not guarantee visa approval.
