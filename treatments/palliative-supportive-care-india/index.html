Palliative and supportive care in India for international patients

Elajme India helps international patients and families coordinate palliative and supportive care enquiries in India. This care focuses on relief of symptoms, quality of life and support for patients and families, alongside other medical treatment when appropriate.

Symptom relief, quality of life and supportive care planning

Specialist assessment

The care team may assess pain, breathlessness, nausea, fatigue and other symptoms alongside the diagnosis, current treatment and their impact on everyday life.

Condition-specific care

Assessment may also consider mobility, sleep, appetite, nutrition, daily activities and the emotional and social needs of the patient and family.

Treatment and follow-up

The treating team develops a supportive-care plan around the patient's condition, care goals and response to treatment, involving other specialties when needed.

Elajme India provides international patient coordination and general information. We do not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment or guarantee treatment acceptance or outcomes.

What to share for an initial review

Useful records may include the diagnosis, recent specialist notes, blood tests, imaging or other relevant investigations, previous treatment and current medicines.

Preparing for a palliative and supportive-care review

What the clinical team reviews

Palliative and supportive care focuses on symptom relief, comfort, quality of life and support alongside the patient’s underlying treatment. Review considers the main diagnosis, current disease status, pain and other symptoms, functional ability, nutrition, emotional needs and the patient’s care goals.

Records that can help

Send the main diagnosis and latest specialist summary, recent imaging and laboratory results where relevant, current and previous treatment details, a complete medication list, recent hospital or discharge records and a short description of the symptoms or practical care needs that currently cause the greatest difficulty.

The treating team confirms the appropriate supportive-care plan after individual review. Elajme India coordinates case submission and communication and does not replace medical advice or emergency care.
