How we can support your case

Medical case coordination

We help organise relevant reports and imaging information for specialist review.

Hospital & specialist communication

We coordinate responses, consultations, appointments and available treatment estimates.

Travel coordination

If you decide to proceed, we assist with practical treatment-visit arrangements.

Neurological and neurosurgical care

The appropriate approach depends on the diagnosis, symptoms, imaging and specialist assessment. Depending on the individual case, a hospital may involve neurology, neurosurgery, neuro-oncology or other specialist services. Elajme India coordinates the enquiry; the medical team determines the investigations and treatment plan.

Preparing your case for review

Relevant records may include recent consultation notes, MRI or CT reports and images, pathology reports where applicable, previous surgery or treatment records and a current medication list. The receiving medical team will confirm what is required.

Medical note: Diagnosis and treatment recommendations must be made by qualified doctors after reviewing the individual patient’s condition and records.
