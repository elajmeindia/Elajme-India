Women’s health coordination

Gynaecology and women’s health treatment in India

Elajme India helps international patients coordinate gynaecology and women’s health enquiries with appropriate hospital teams in India. Diagnosis, investigations and treatment depend on individual specialist assessment.

Request coordination through Elajme India

Care depends on the individual condition

Gynaecological conditions

Specialist assessment may be relevant for menstrual disorders, fibroids, ovarian or uterine conditions, pelvic symptoms and other gynaecological concerns.

Gynaecological surgery

When surgery is being considered, the treating team determines whether a procedure is appropriate and which surgical approach may be suitable after evaluation.

Fertility-related care

Patients whose main concern is fertility or assisted reproduction can also review our dedicated IVF and fertility pathway.

IVF & Fertility Care
Elajme India provides coordination and general information. We do not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment or guarantee clinical outcomes.

What to share for an initial review

Useful records may include the diagnosis or main concern, recent consultation notes, ultrasound or other imaging, pathology or laboratory reports, previous procedures, relevant medical history and current medicines.

1. Send available records

Share the main medical concern and relevant available records with Elajme India.

2. Coordinate specialist review

We can coordinate submission to an appropriate gynaecology or women’s health team for preliminary review.

3. Confirm next steps

Consultation availability, additional investigations, treatment options, timing and estimates remain subject to the treating provider’s review.
