Pediatric care coordination

Pediatrics and child health in India for international patients

Elajme India helps families coordinate paediatric consultations in India for children with general or specialist health needs. The treating team reviews the child's age, symptoms, growth, medical history and relevant reports before advising on assessment and care.

Child health assessment and specialist paediatric care

Pediatric assessment

The pediatric team reviews the child’s symptoms, diagnosis, growth and development, previous treatment and relevant medical history.

Specialist pediatric care

Depending on the condition, the child may be reviewed by general pediatrics or an appropriate pediatric subspecialty team.

Family-centred follow-up

The treating team explains investigations, treatment and follow-up requirements to the child’s parent or legal guardian.

Elajme India provides international patient coordination and general information. We do not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment or guarantee treatment acceptance or outcomes.

What to share for an initial review

Useful records may include the child’s diagnosis, age, growth information, recent pediatric notes, blood tests, imaging, discharge summaries, vaccination history when relevant, previous treatment and current medicines.

1. Send available records

Share the diagnosis, current medical concern and relevant available records with Elajme India.

2. Coordinate specialist review

We can coordinate submission to an appropriate hospital or specialist team for preliminary review.

3. Confirm next steps

Investigations, treatment options, timing, eligibility and estimates remain subject to the treating provider’s review.

Preparing for a paediatric specialist review

What the clinical team reviews

Children require age-appropriate assessment based on growth, development, symptoms, previous diagnoses, vaccinations, medicines and the specific organ system involved. The appropriate paediatric subspecialty depends on the individual concern.

Records that can help

Send the child’s age, weight and main concern, recent paediatric notes, growth or developmental information when relevant, laboratory and imaging reports, previous admissions or procedures, vaccination information and current medicines.

The treating team confirms diagnosis, suitability, investigations and treatment after individual review. Elajme India coordinates case submission and communication and does not replace medical advice.
