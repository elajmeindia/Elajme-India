Oncology coordination

Cancer treatment in India for international patients

Elajme India helps international patients and families coordinate cancer-care enquiries with hospitals and oncology teams in India, including medical-record review, consultation requests, available treatment information and practical treatment-visit planning.

Request coordination through Elajme India

Cancer care is individual to each patient

The appropriate approach depends on the cancer type, stage, pathology, previous treatment and the patient's overall health. A qualified oncology team must review the individual case and recommend the treatment plan.

Surgery

Surgery may form part of treatment for selected solid tumours depending on specialist assessment.

Systemic treatment

Chemotherapy, targeted therapy or immunotherapy may be considered in selected cases according to the diagnosis and clinical findings.

Radiation therapy

Radiation therapy may be considered alone or together with other treatment approaches when clinically appropriate.

This page provides general coordination information and is not medical advice. Elajme India does not diagnose cancer, select treatment or guarantee outcomes.

What to share for preliminary review

Useful records may include a recent medical summary, pathology or biopsy reports, imaging reports and images when available, previous treatment records, current medicines and recent laboratory reports.

1. Share the case

Send the diagnosis or main concern and relevant available medical records.

2. Hospital coordination

We coordinate the enquiry with an appropriate hospital or oncology team for review.

3. Review next steps

When medical feedback is available, we help coordinate consultations and practical treatment-visit requirements.
