Profile overview

Medanta currently lists Dr. Naresh Trehan as Chairman, Managing Director and Chief Cardiac Surgeon. Medanta states that he has more than 57 years of experience in cardiac surgery and has performed more than 48,000 open-heart surgeries. His listed expertise includes coronary artery bypass surgery, heart valve repair and replacement, aortic surgery, complex and redo cardiac surgery, minimally invasive and robotically assisted cardiac surgery, and other advanced cardiovascular procedures.

His listed qualifications and training include MBBS from King George’s Medical College, University of Lucknow, Diplomate of the American Board of Surgery, Diplomate of the American Board of Thoracic Surgery, advanced surgical training at Thomas Jefferson University Hospital, and thoracic and cardiac surgery training and faculty tenure at New York University Medical Center.
