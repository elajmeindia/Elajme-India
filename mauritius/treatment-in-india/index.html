Prepare your referral from Mauritius

Local assessment or referral

Collect the letter from your Mauritius clinic or hospital, the purpose of referral and relevant past treatment. Ask the referring clinician to state what needs to be reviewed in India.

English and French documentation

Keep the original French compte rendu and laboratory documents. Ask the Indian hospital whether it needs an English version, who should prepare it and whether French interpretation is available at appointments.

Imaging and treatment record

For cancer care, collect relevant pathology and information on previous surgery, medicines or radiotherapy. For orthopaedic or eye care, include the specialist assessment, recent test results and any original images the hospital requests.

Share only the records needed

Use the enquiry form to request next steps or ask on WhatsApp how to send files for hospital review. If you are handling a family member’s referral, confirm their permission before sharing it.

Start a hospital enquiry

Compare Indian hospital plans and prepare for admission

Identify the specialist

Use the referral to compare departments and ask which doctor will review the individual case. Check whether diagnostic tests and follow-up services are available at the same campus.

Request a review and written estimate

Ask whether the hospital offers remote review and whether there is a fee. Request a clear account of the proposed tests, procedures, hospital days and services excluded from the estimate.

Confirm the appointment and admission process

Ask for the hospital address, appointment date, deposit and documents to bring. Check whether assessment and admission are separate and when a companion can visit or stay.

Plan discharge before leaving Mauritius

Arrange for your referring team to receive the discharge summary, test results and follow-up plan. Ask whether the India hospital can offer a remote review after return and how to arrange it.

Request a hospital estimate in its billing currency and budget for tests, specialist fees, procedures, medicines and additional hospital days. Calculate flights, accommodation, meals, local transfers and companion expenses separately. If using insurance or other funding, confirm the written approval, exclusions and reimbursement method. Ask your bank about MUR-to-INR conversion and international payment charges.

Indian medical visa and travel planning from Mauritius

Choose the official visa process

Mauritius appears on the Indian e-Visa eligibility list. Confirm that your passport and case meet the e-Medical conditions. For a regular private medical visit, the High Commission in Port Louis lists an Indian hospital letter, evidence of a prior medical check or referral in Mauritius, and proof of sufficient funds.

Confirm the hospital letter and companion documents

The High Commission checklist asks for patient and companion details in the hospital letter where applicable. Check the current passport and photo requirements and whether the companion needs a separate Medical Attendant application. Ask the authority whether translations are required for any visa documents.

Confirm the hospital city before purchasing flights

Check current routes from Mauritius and eligible arrival points for your visa. Confirm transit rules with the carrier. Choose accommodation by travel time to the hospital campus and ask about transport, accessibility and late arrival arrangements.

The e-Medical application checklist includes a passport scan and a letter from the Indian hospital with a proposed admission date. Verify all current uploads, English-language document requirements and the separate attendant category on the portal.

Questions about treatment in India from Mauritius

Can I request a second opinion before booking from Mauritius?

Yes. We can ask an appropriate hospital department about remote review. Ask whether the specialist can review the referral remotely and how to provide scans and past results.

Which documents does Port Louis list for a private medical visit?

The High Commission lists an Indian hospital letter, evidence of a check-up or referral in Mauritius, and proof of sufficient funds. Confirm the latest full checklist and application process with the official source.

Will my French medical reports be accepted?

The reviewing specialist team decides which formats and languages it can use. Keep your originals and confirm whether the team wants a complete translation or only selected reports. Check visa translations separately.

How should I compare the cost of treatment in India?

Request written estimates for the same scope. Check diagnostic tests, fees, medicines, room type, estimated admission days and excluded items. Ask what would change after assessment or if the treatment plan changes.

Can a relative travel with me and stay nearby?

Check the applicable attendant category, documentation and costs before booking. Ask about hospital visitor rules, family accommodation and the time needed to get between accommodation and the hospital.

How long should I stay after treatment?

Ask the clinical team to distinguish inpatient days, local recovery and follow-up appointments. Confirm when it expects to assess fitness to fly before purchasing a fixed return ticket.

How do I transfer care back to Mauritius?

Ask for the discharge summary, procedure or treatment details, medication plan and follow-up dates. Confirm who will review these in Mauritius and which questions should go back to the Indian team.
