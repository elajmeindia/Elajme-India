Not sure which specialty is appropriate?

Share the diagnosis, main medical concern or available reports with Elajme India. We can help route the enquiry to a relevant hospital team for preliminary review. The treating medical team determines the appropriate specialty, investigations and treatment.

What happens after you send your case?

Share the diagnosis or main medical concern, recent reports and scans, previous treatment records and your country. Elajme India can coordinate submission to an appropriate hospital or specialist team for preliminary review. After review, the treating team confirms the relevant specialty, any additional investigations, consultation availability and proposed next steps.
