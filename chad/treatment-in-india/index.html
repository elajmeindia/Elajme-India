Medical treatment in India for patients from Chad

How to submit medical reports from Chad

Referral and history

Start with a referral letter or specialist summary, your current medicines and details of any prior operation or treatment. Include your city and the specialty you are seeking, if known.

Test results and images

Include relevant recent lab results and dated imaging reports. If scans are on a disc or in large files, ask how to share them through a channel the hospital accepts.

Records in French or Arabic

Tell us which language your records use before paying for a translation. Keep the originals and ask which pages need an English translation for the hospital to review.

Permission to share

If you are contacting us for a relative, obtain their permission to share their records. Use the enquiry form to start, or ask on WhatsApp how to send the reports.

What Elajme India can coordinate before you travel

Identify the specialty

We use the referral and your priorities to direct the enquiry to a relevant hospital department in India.

Request a review

We can ask whether a specialist can review your records or offer a remote consultation. The hospital may request additional records or an in-person assessment.

Ask for a written estimate

Request an itemised estimate and ask which tests, procedures and follow-up visits it covers. Check the currency, payment terms and how long the estimate is valid.

Plan the visit

If you decide to proceed, we can coordinate appointment details and discuss practical arrangements such as transfers and accommodation near the confirmed hospital.

An estimate is preliminary. Ask separately about medicines after discharge, extra hospital days, accommodation and your companion’s expenses. The hospital sets the final treatment plan and charges after assessment.

Medical visa and travel planning from Chad

Confirm the visa route

For a Chadian passport, consult the Indian Embassy in N’Djamena about the medical visa application, the hospital documents needed and the separate requirements for an accompanying person. Use the official regular visa portal for application instructions.

Check yellow fever documentation

India’s Directorate General of Health Services lists Chad among yellow fever risk countries. Check the certificate requirements for the patient and companion with the airline and Indian arrival health authority, including any transit stops. Ask a travel health clinician about individual eligibility.

Book around the confirmed hospital

Confirm your appointment city and arrival date first. If departing from N’Djamena, verify your connections and any transit visa rules with the carrier before paying. Ask your treating clinician about fitness to fly if assistance may be needed.

Questions patients in Chad ask before travelling

Can I get a second opinion in India while I am still in Chad?

Elajme India can request a record review or remote consultation where the specialist offers it. The hospital decides whether it can give an opinion from the records and confirms any consultation fee.

Do I have to translate my French reports first?

Keep your original French reports. Ask us to check the hospital’s language requirements before ordering a translation. If you need French interpretation during an appointment, ask about availability and cost in advance.

How much does it cost to travel from Chad to India for treatment?

Start with a hospital estimate based on your case, then budget separately for flights, visas, accommodation, local transport and a companion if applicable. Ask which hospital charges could change after an in-person evaluation.

Can my relative accompany me to India?

Ask the Indian Embassy in N’Djamena which application and supporting documents your companion needs. Ask the hospital what documents it can provide after reviewing your case.

Can I arrange follow-up from Chad after returning home?

Before leaving India, ask the treating team for a discharge summary, written follow-up instructions and any remote review options. Share the plan with the clinician who will follow your care in Chad.

What to confirm before booking from Chad

Visa and attendant documents

Before booking, confirm with the Indian mission in N’Djamena which hospital documents and attendant papers apply. Check yellow fever documentation for the entire itinerary, including transit, using the official sources listed above.

What does the estimate include?

Ask the hospital for the billing currency, deposit and cancellation rules. When comparing estimates from Chad, confirm whether diagnostic imaging, medicines and extra hospital days are included, and keep local transfers and companion expenses separate.

Admission, recovery and follow-up

Ask which English translations the hospital wants before admission. For the return to Chad, confirm whether the local clinician needs a translated discharge summary, treatment records or imaging files and arrange the handover before leaving India.
