Individual specialist assessment matters

Urology and kidney conditions can require different specialists and investigations. Treatment suitability depends on the individual diagnosis and specialist assessment.

Medical note: Elajme India coordinates patient enquiries and does not make diagnoses or determine treatment suitability.
