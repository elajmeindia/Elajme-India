How the coordination process works

1. Share your enquiry

Tell us the treatment or medical specialty you are looking for and share relevant reports when requested.

2. Hospital review

We coordinate your case with relevant hospitals or medical teams for review and available treatment information.

3. Plan your visit

If you choose to proceed, we assist with practical coordination related to appointments, travel and stay.
