Endocrinology and diabetes care in India for international patients

If you are seeking diabetes, thyroid or hormonal care in India, Elajme India can help coordinate your enquiry with endocrinology specialists. Your treating team determines the appropriate investigations and management plan after reviewing your individual condition.

Diabetes, thyroid and hormonal conditions requiring specialist review

Clinical assessment

The specialist reviews symptoms, diagnosis, previous treatment, relevant laboratory results and overall medical history.

Diabetes and metabolic care

The treating team determines appropriate investigations and an individualized management plan for diabetes and metabolic conditions.

Thyroid and hormonal care

Thyroid and other hormonal concerns are assessed according to symptoms, examination and test results.

Elajme India provides international patient coordination and general information. We do not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment or guarantee treatment acceptance or outcomes.

What to share for an initial review

Useful records may include recent blood tests, glucose and HbA1c results, thyroid or hormone tests, relevant imaging, previous diagnoses, treatment history and current medicines.

1. Send available records

Share the diagnosis, current medical concern and relevant available records with Elajme India.

2. Coordinate specialist review

We can coordinate submission to an appropriate hospital or specialist team for preliminary review.

3. Confirm next steps

Investigations, treatment options, timing, eligibility and estimates remain subject to the treating provider’s review.

Preparing for an endocrinology review

What the clinical team reviews

Endocrine care may involve diabetes, thyroid, adrenal, pituitary, metabolic or other hormonal conditions. Assessment depends on symptoms, laboratory trends, imaging when relevant, medicines and previous specialist findings.

Records that can help

Send recent blood tests relevant to the condition, glucose and HbA1c records for diabetes, thyroid or hormone results where applicable, imaging reports, previous endocrinology notes and a current medication list.

The treating team confirms diagnosis, suitability, investigations and treatment after individual review. Elajme India coordinates case submission and communication and does not replace medical advice.
