When a gastroenterology review may be needed

Patients may seek specialist review for persistent digestive symptoms, liver or pancreatic concerns, inflammatory bowel conditions, gastrointestinal bleeding, swallowing problems, abnormal investigations or a previously advised endoscopy or procedure. The appropriate specialist, tests and treatment depend on the individual case.

Digestive and intestinal conditions

Specialist review can help assess symptoms and existing findings involving the oesophagus, stomach, small intestine or colon.

Liver, gallbladder and pancreas

Gastroenterology and related specialist teams may review liver disease, biliary conditions and pancreatic disorders depending on the diagnosis.

Endoscopy and procedures

Where clinically appropriate, a specialist may advise diagnostic or therapeutic endoscopic procedures after reviewing the patient’s history and investigations.

Important: Elajme India coordinates communication and patient support. We do not diagnose a condition, prescribe treatment or determine whether a procedure is suitable.

What to share for preliminary hospital review

Where available, share a recent medical summary, current symptoms and diagnosis, blood-test results, ultrasound/CT/MRI reports and images, endoscopy or colonoscopy reports, biopsy or pathology reports if relevant, previous treatment or surgery records and a current medication list. The hospital may request additional information.

Frequently asked questions

Can I request a medical opinion before travelling?

Where a hospital or specialist offers remote case review, Elajme India can coordinate the available reports for preliminary feedback. A remote review may not replace an in-person examination or additional tests.

Can the hospital estimate treatment cost before arrival?

An indicative estimate may sometimes be available after record review. The final cost can change after clinical evaluation, investigations, procedure choice, length of stay and other patient-specific requirements.

Does every digestive problem need an endoscopy?

No. The need for endoscopy, imaging, laboratory tests or another investigation is determined by the treating specialist after assessing the individual case.
