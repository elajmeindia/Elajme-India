Preparing for specialist review

Clinical assessment

Review may consider the suspected infection, duration and severity, microbiology results, imaging, immune status, travel or exposure history and response to previous treatment.

Useful records

Send culture or PCR results, recent blood tests, imaging, hospital notes, previous antimicrobial treatment details and current medicines where available.

Elajme India provides international patient coordination and general information. We do not diagnose, prescribe treatment or guarantee acceptance or outcomes.
