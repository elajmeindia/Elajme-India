International patient coordination

Case review

We help organise and coordinate relevant medical information for hospital review.

Hospital communication

We help coordinate consultations, appointments and available hospital estimates.

Visit planning

Practical support is available for treatment-related travel and local coordination.

Preparing for a hospital opinion

The medical team may request recent consultation notes, laboratory results, imaging, pathology reports where applicable, previous treatment records and current medications. Requirements vary by condition and hospital.

Before you arrange travel

It is sensible to obtain case-specific hospital guidance before finalising a treatment visit. We can coordinate practical next steps after review. Visa and entry approvals remain the responsibility of the relevant authorities.
