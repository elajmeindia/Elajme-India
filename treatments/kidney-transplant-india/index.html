Kidney transplant coordination in India

Elajme India helps international patients coordinate kidney transplant enquiries with transplant hospitals and specialist teams in India. Transplant eligibility, donor evaluation and all required approvals depend on medical assessment and applicable laws and hospital protocols.

How we can support the coordination process

Case coordination

Help organise relevant patient and donor medical information for hospital review.

Hospital communication

Coordinate responses, consultations and information requested by the transplant team.

Visit planning

Support practical treatment-visit arrangements after the hospital advises the appropriate next steps.

Important: Elajme India does not determine transplant eligibility, donor suitability or legal approval. These decisions are made by qualified medical teams and relevant authorities under applicable requirements.
