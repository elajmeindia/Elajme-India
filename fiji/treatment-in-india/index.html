Medical treatment in India for patients from Fiji

If you have a referral from Suva, Lautoka or elsewhere in Fiji, request an Indian hospital review before committing to a long journey. Elajme India can coordinate a specialist enquiry, ask for an itemised estimate and help you clarify the appointment, travel and follow-up steps.

Prepare your referral in Fiji

Ask for a dated referral

Include the condition being assessed, past procedures, recent tests, current medicines and the question your clinician wants an Indian specialist to address.

Organise essential scans and results

Keep the original files and reports. For a heart, orthopaedic or eye referral, ask which tests must be supplied in full and how to share large files.

Start the case securely

Use the enquiry form to introduce the case or ask on WhatsApp how to submit the reports. Obtain the patient’s consent if submitting for a relative.

Start your medical enquiry

Compare hospitals and plan the full visit

Check the specialist and campus

Match the hospital department to your referral. Ask which clinician will see you, where diagnostics are performed and whether the hospital offers a remote appointment.

Request a written scope of care

Ask the hospital for estimated diagnostic, specialist, procedure, room and medicine charges. Confirm what could be added after assessment and how deposits and rescheduling are handled.

Schedule admission and recovery

Ask for separate dates or timeframes for consultation, admission, local recovery and review. Discuss fitness to fly with your treating team before booking a fixed return.

Prepare a handover for Fiji

Ask for the discharge summary, relevant results, medication list and any follow-up schedule. Confirm who in Fiji can receive the records and whether the Indian specialist can review progress remotely.

Keep the hospital estimate in its billing currency and budget separately for connecting flights, visas, meals, accommodation and transfers for you and any attendant. Ask your bank about FJD-to-INR currency and payment fees. Final hospital charges depend on the assessment and care delivered.

Request a hospital estimate

Specialties to discuss with your referring clinician

For heart care, gather available cardiac tests. For an orthopaedic opinion, ask about imaging, non-surgical alternatives and the rehabilitation plan. For oncology or eye care, check who will review the records and whether repeated testing or further visits might be required.

Medical visa and travel from Fiji

Check e-Medical eligibility

Fiji is listed on India’s official e-Visa portal. Confirm your individual passport eligibility, hospital letter and arrival port. Review the separate e-Medical Attendant category if travelling with a companion.

Check the official Suva guidance

The High Commission in Suva publishes visa application information. For a regular medical visa or a case raising additional documentation questions, confirm the current checklist with the High Commission before booking.

Plan the full route

If leaving from Nadi, compare the complete itinerary to the hospital city. Check transit permissions and baggage arrangements, especially when connections are on separate tickets. Verify accessible transfers and accommodation near the confirmed campus.

The e-Medical application checklist includes a passport scan and a letter from the Indian hospital with a proposed admission date. Verify all current uploads, English-language document requirements and the separate attendant category on the portal.

Patient questions before travelling from Fiji

Can I ask for a second opinion from Fiji?

Yes. We can request a review and ask about remote appointments. Confirm the consultation fee and the reports needed before travel.

Can I compare quotes before choosing a hospital?

Ask for written quotes covering comparable procedures, investigation, room type, days of admission and follow-up. The plan and costs may change after examination.

What should I check if a relative is travelling with me?

Check official guidance for the attendant application and ask the hospital about its visitor policy and companion accommodation. Budget separately for their travel and stay.

How much time should I plan for India?

Ask about assessment, admission, recovery and follow-up days separately. Confirm when the doctor will reassess whether the return journey is appropriate.

Can my clinician in Fiji continue the follow-up?

Ask the treating team which visits and tests can be handled locally and how your Fiji clinician can contact the Indian team if needed.
