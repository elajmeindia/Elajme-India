Medical treatment in India for patients from Nigeria

Elajme India helps Nigerian patients and families coordinate treatment enquiries with hospitals and medical teams in India. We support the journey from initial case review and hospital communication to appointment and treatment-visit planning.

How Elajme India can support your treatment journey

Medical case coordination

We help organise relevant reports and coordinate your case with appropriate hospitals or specialist teams.

Hospital & appointment support

We coordinate hospital communication, available consultations, appointments and treatment estimates.

Travel & local coordination

If you decide to proceed, we can assist with practical arrangements connected with your treatment visit to India.
