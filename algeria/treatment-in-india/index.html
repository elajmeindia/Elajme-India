Medical visa documents for patients in Algeria

Check the Algiers embassy checklist

The Embassy of India in Algiers lists English translations of reports from a local doctor for its medical visa file. This is a separate step from asking a hospital to review your records.

Ask the hospital about the invitation

The embassy checklist calls for an invitation from an eligible Indian provider with the patient’s and attendant’s passport details. Ask the hospital when it can issue the letter and confirm the current checklist with the embassy.

Check the journey from your departure city

Confirm the Indian appointment city and the embassy’s booking-document requirements before paying non-refundable fares. Check any transit requirements with the carrier.

Questions about arranging treatment from Algeria

Can I send a French compte rendu for a second opinion?

Tell us what records you have and keep the original. We can check which reports the hospital needs translated. The embassy lists English translations separately for the medical visa file.

Can I speak with an Indian specialist before travelling?

We can ask whether the selected specialist offers a remote review for your case and confirm any appointment fee and interpretation availability with the hospital.

Will I know the hospital charges before I leave Algeria?

We can request a preliminary written estimate. Ask which items are included, when the estimate expires and how the hospital handles changes after examination.

What if my companion needs a visa too?

Check the embassy’s requirements for the companion’s application. Ask the hospital how it records the companion’s details when preparing a visa letter.

What should I take back to my specialist in Algeria?

Ask for the discharge summary, relevant test results, a medicine list and the follow-up plan. Confirm with both teams which records may need translating.

What to confirm before booking from Algeria

Visa and attendant documents

The embassy’s medical visa checklist distinguishes the English translation of the local medical report from the hospital’s own review. Confirm its latest booking and supporting-document rules before making non-refundable purchases.

What does the estimate include?

For a planned procedure, request a written scope covering anaesthesia, room, follow-up and rehabilitation. Ask which items the hospital would add if it changes the plan after assessment, how to pay from Algeria and how refunds are handled.

Admission, recovery and follow-up

Confirm the appointment campus and dates for assessment, admission and expected local recovery. Before discharge, work out which reports your clinician in Algeria needs in French and who should arrange translations.
