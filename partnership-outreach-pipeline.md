# Elajme India — Hospital & Doctor Partnership Outreach Pipeline

This document defines a practical outreach pipeline for developing verified hospital and doctor coordination relationships for international patients.

## Partnership objective
Build reliable working relationships with appropriate Indian hospitals and specialist teams so Elajme India can coordinate international patient enquiries efficiently while keeping medical decisions with qualified providers.

A website listing does not imply a formal partnership, endorsement or guaranteed acceptance unless a relationship has actually been confirmed.

## Priority partner types
1. International patient departments of hospitals already represented on Elajme India.
2. Hospital coordinators handling GCC/MEA or Arabic-speaking patients.
3. Specialist departments relevant to Elajme India's nine treatment categories.
4. Verified specialist practices where coordination can be handled through an authorized hospital/clinic channel.

## Priority treatment categories
- Cancer
- Heart treatment
- Gastroenterology
- Kidney transplant
- Knee replacement / orthopaedics
- Liver transplant
- Neurosurgery
- Spine surgery
- Urology

## Outreach stages
1. Target Identified
2. Contact Verified
3. Outreach Sent
4. Follow-up Due
5. Discussion Started
6. Coordination Process Confirmed
7. Active Referral/Coordination Relationship
8. Paused / Not Proceeding

## Private partner-tracking fields
Keep the operational tracker in a private CRM/workspace rather than this public repository. Recommended fields:
- Partner organization
- City
- Hospital/clinic department
- Specialty/treatment categories
- Contact person's professional role
- Official business contact channel
- Date first contacted
- Outreach source
- Current stage
- Last interaction date
- Next follow-up date
- Coordination process/status
- Notes on required case-submission format

Do not publish private contact details, commercial terms, patient information or confidential partner communications in the public repository.

## Initial outreach proposition
Elajme India can introduce itself as an international patient coordination service focused on Arabic- and English-speaking patients, particularly from GCC/MEA markets. Outreach should emphasize:
- structured collection and coordination of patient enquiries
- Arabic/English communication support
- pre-travel report coordination
- appointment/consultation coordination
- patient journey support
- a single Elajme coordination channel for the patient

Do not claim existing patient volume, formal hospital relationships, exclusive arrangements or guaranteed conversions unless documented and true.

## What to confirm with a hospital/partner
Before treating a relationship as operational, confirm:
- official international-patient/contact channel
- how medical reports should be submitted securely
- specialties/cases the team handles
- expected process for medical review
- how consultation availability is communicated
- how hospital estimates are issued
- medical visa invitation/support-document process where applicable
- escalation/contact route for an active patient case
- whether any referral/commercial arrangement exists and its documented terms

## Outreach cadence
Suggested internal sequence:
- Day 0: initial professional introduction
- Day 3–5: first follow-up if no response
- Day 8–12: second follow-up
- After that: pause unless there is a reasonable new reason to contact the organization

This is an internal operating cadence, not a promise to patients or partners.

## Partner quality checks
Prefer partners with:
- verifiable hospital/clinic identity
- relevant specialty capability
- a functioning international-patient or patient-coordination process
- clear professional communication
- secure method for receiving medical records
- transparent hospital-issued estimates/process information where applicable

Avoid presenting unverified intermediaries as hospitals or medical providers.

## Patient-routing rule
All website consultation enquiries continue to route through Elajme India. Elajme may coordinate a case with a selected hospital or doctor, but diagnosis, treatment recommendations, acceptance, appointment availability, final cost and clinical outcomes remain with the qualified medical provider.

## Partnership KPIs
Track privately:
- verified contacts added
- outreach sent
- response rate
- discussions started
- coordination processes confirmed
- active hospital/specialist relationships
- qualified patient cases referred/coordinated
- response time on active case reviews

## First implementation batch
Start with hospitals and specialist teams already represented on Elajme India rather than expanding the public directory solely for outreach. Prioritize Delhi NCR and Mumbai/Navi Mumbai institutions already used across treatment journeys, then expand based on patient demand and partner responsiveness.
