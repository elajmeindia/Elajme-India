Medical visa planning for treatment in India

International patients may need appropriate travel permission for medical treatment in India. Requirements can vary by nationality, circumstances and current government rules.

Ask about treatment planning

Plan after medical review

Where possible, obtain case-specific guidance from the hospital or treating team before finalising travel. Depending on the case, hospital documentation may form part of the patient's travel or visa preparation.

Documents to prepare

For an e-Medical Visa, the Government of India currently lists the passport bio page and a letter from the hospital in India that includes the proposed or tentative admission or treatment date. The hospital letter should identify the patient, including the name, nationality and passport number. Requirements can change, so confirm the current checklist on the official Indian e-Visa website before applying.

When to prepare the e-Medical Visa application

For eligible nationalities, the Government of India currently states that e-Medical and e-Medical Attendant applications may be submitted at least four days before the proposed arrival date and within the application window shown on the official e-Visa portal. Because eligibility and procedures can change, patients should check the official portal for their nationality before making travel commitments.

Medical attendants

The official e-Visa guidance provides an e-Medical Attendant category for eligible attendants accompanying an e-Medical Visa holder and currently states that up to two e-Medical Attendant visas may be granted against one e-Medical Visa. Current eligibility and document requirements should always be checked on the government portal before travel planning.

Passport and travel preparation

The official e-Visa guidance currently requires an eligible applicant's passport to have at least six months validity when applying and at least two blank pages. It also states that international travellers should have a return or onward journey ticket and sufficient funds for their stay. These are government requirements and should be rechecked before application and travel.

Check current official requirements

Visa categories, eligibility, documentation and processing procedures are determined by the relevant Indian government authorities and can change. Apply and verify the latest requirements through the official Government of India e-Visa portal or the Indian embassy, high commission or consulate serving your country.

How Elajme India can help

We can coordinate with the hospital regarding available case-review documents and help patients understand practical treatment-visit steps. We do not issue visas, submit visa applications on behalf of government authorities, provide immigration decisions or guarantee visa approval.

Important: Do not make non-refundable travel arrangements solely on the basis of preliminary treatment information. Hospital acceptance, medical plans, costs and visa decisions can depend on individual circumstances and further review.
