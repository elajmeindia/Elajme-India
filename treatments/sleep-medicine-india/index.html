Sleep pattern, daytime symptoms, breathing during sleep, medical conditions, medicines and previous sleep testing can all affect the clinical pathway.

Send previous sleep-study results if available, ENT or respiratory notes where relevant, CPAP or other device information, medical history and current medicines.
