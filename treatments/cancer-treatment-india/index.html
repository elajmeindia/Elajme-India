Cancer treatment in India

Elajme India helps international patients and families organise oncology enquiries with hospitals and specialist teams in India. We coordinate medical-record sharing, consultation requests, hospital responses and practical treatment-visit planning while medical decisions remain with the treating oncology team.

Cancer care is individual to each patient

The appropriate approach depends on the cancer type, stage, pathology, previous treatment, test results and the patient's overall health. A qualified oncology team must review the individual case before recommending treatment.

Surgery

Surgery may form part of treatment for selected solid tumours depending on specialist assessment.

Systemic treatment

Chemotherapy, targeted therapy or immunotherapy may be considered in selected cases according to diagnosis and clinical findings.

Radiation therapy

Radiation therapy may be considered alone or together with other approaches when clinically appropriate.

Haematology care

Blood cancers may require evaluation by a haematology or haemato-oncology team, with treatment determined after specialist review.

This page provides general coordination information and is not medical advice. Elajme India does not diagnose cancer, prescribe treatment, rank doctors or hospitals, or guarantee treatment outcomes.

What to share for a preliminary case review

You do not need to organise every document before contacting us. Send the records you currently have, and the hospital or medical team may request additional information after reviewing the case.

Diagnosis and pathology

Recent medical summary, biopsy or pathology reports and relevant laboratory results when available.

Imaging

CT, MRI, PET-CT or other relevant imaging reports and images when available.

Previous treatment

Details of surgery, chemotherapy, radiation, other cancer treatment and current medicines.

How Elajme India coordinates your enquiry

1. Share your case

Submit the patient's basic details, diagnosis or main concern and available medical records through the Elajme India enquiry channel.

2. Case routing

We coordinate the enquiry with a relevant hospital or medical team based on the information supplied. Hospital acceptance and specialist availability are confirmed case by case.

3. Medical feedback

When feedback is received, we help communicate the available consultation pathway, requested investigations and hospital-provided treatment information or estimates.

4. Visit coordination

If the patient plans to travel, we can assist with coordination around appointments and practical treatment-visit requirements. Visa decisions remain with the relevant authorities.

Frequently asked questions

Can I request a second opinion?

You can submit available records for coordination of a consultation or case review. Whether a formal second opinion can be provided depends on the hospital, specialist and information available.

Can you confirm the treatment cost before travel?

Hospitals may provide an indicative estimate after reviewing the case. Final costs can change depending on clinical findings, investigations, treatment decisions, hospital stay and other services.

Do I need a confirmed treatment plan before contacting you?

No. You can share the diagnosis, main medical concern and available reports. The treating medical team determines appropriate next steps after review.

Does Elajme India provide medical advice?

No. Elajme India coordinates communication and patient-support arrangements. Diagnosis, treatment recommendations and clinical decisions are made by qualified healthcare professionals.
