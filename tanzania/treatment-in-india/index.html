What to send for a specialist review from Tanzania

Referral and current care

Send a recent referral or specialist summary, diagnosis if known, current medicines, prior procedures and the question you want reviewed. Include your city and identify your referring doctor or clinic if relevant.

Reports and original images

Gather dated lab results, discharge notes and imaging reports. For an orthopaedic or neurological review, the hospital may need the image files. Ask how to transmit large files and keep originals for your appointment.

Kiswahili-language records

Keep original records. If your documents are in Kiswahili, ask the receiving hospital which pages need an English translation and whether it requires a particular format. Confirm interpreter availability with the hospital before booking.

Share through an agreed channel

Start with basic information using the enquiry form. Ask on WhatsApp how to share reports for hospital review. If you are helping a relative, get their permission before sharing records.

Request an Indian hospital review and treatment estimate

Identify a suitable department

We route your enquiry based on the referral, question and records available. Ask who would review the case and whether a clinician with the relevant specialty is available.

Ask for a preliminary plan

The hospital may review records, offer a video consultation or ask for more tests. Clarify what can be decided before travel and what requires examination in India.

Compare written quotes

Ask for the bill in Indian rupees, the deposit amount and refund terms. Compare estimates that cover the same procedures, tests and expected hospital days.

Coordinate admission and follow-up

Confirm the consultation location, admission documents, expected inpatient and nearby recovery days, and how your Tanzanian doctor will receive the discharge plan.

Budget separately for repeat tests and imaging, specialist and anaesthesia fees, hospital stay, medicines, rehabilitation, flights, visas, accommodation and local transport. Ask your bank about TZS-to-INR conversion and card limits. Hospital estimates can change after clinical assessment or if additional care is required.

Indian medical visa and travel from Tanzania

Check the medical visa route

Tanzania appears on the Indian e-Visa eligibility list. Confirm that your passport and case meet the e-Medical conditions. For a regular application, the Indian High Commission in Dar es Salaam lists preliminary medical advice or an Indian appointment letter, and evidence of financial standing.

Prepare hospital and attendant paperwork

Ask the Indian hospital what letter it can issue after case review. Check the official visa checklist for passport, photo, medical evidence and any separate Medical Attendant application. Confirm registration or extension requirements with the mission if your stay changes.

Plan transport around the appointment

Check routes from your departure airport, such as Dar es Salaam or Kilimanjaro, to the hospital city. Verify connection times, transit visas, yellow fever documentation for the itinerary, and wheelchair assistance if needed. Confirm nearby accommodation and the transfer time from the airport before booking.

The e-Medical application checklist includes a passport scan and a letter from the Indian hospital with a proposed admission date. Verify all current uploads, English-language document requirements and the separate attendant category on the portal.

Questions before travelling to India from Tanzania

Can a specialist review my case while I am in Tanzania?

We can request a document review or remote consultation if the hospital offers one. Ask whether the doctor can give a preliminary opinion, which reports are essential and what the consultation costs.

Which Indian hospital should I choose for heart or kidney care?

Start with the department your doctor recommends. Compare the clinician’s specialty, relevant facilities, planned tests and access to follow-up. Ask the hospital what it would need to confirm an appointment.

How much does medical treatment in India cost from Tanzania?

Request an estimate based on your records and separate hospital charges from flights and living costs. Ask what the hospital excludes, what might change after testing, and how it handles payment and cancellations.

Can I use records in Kiswahili?

Keep the original and ask the hospital which parts need an English translation. If the records will also support a visa application, check translation requirements with the visa authority.

Can someone accompany me on a medical trip?

Check the Medical Attendant visa route with the official portal or Indian High Commission. Ask the hospital to confirm what companion information it needs, and budget for the companion’s flights, visa and stay.

How long should I plan to stay in India?

Ask for a schedule that separates tests, inpatient admission, recovery near the hospital and follow-up visits. Your team may revise the schedule after examination. Ask when the treating clinician expects to assess fitness for air travel.

What should I take home to my doctor?

Request the discharge summary, relevant reports, a current medicines list, follow-up instructions and a contact for queries. Confirm which follow-up can be handled locally and when the Indian team should review your case again.
