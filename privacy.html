Privacy Policy

How Elajme India handles information shared by international patients and their families.

1. About this policy

Elajme India provides healthcare facilitation and international patient coordination services for people seeking treatment in India. This policy explains how information shared with us may be used for coordination.

Important: Elajme India is not a hospital or medical provider. Medical advice, diagnosis and treatment decisions are made by qualified healthcare professionals.

2. Information you may share

You may provide your name, country, age, mobile or WhatsApp number, preferred language, medical condition, treatment requirement and relevant medical reports or documents.

3. How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, communicate with you and, where appropriate, coordinate your case with relevant hospitals, doctors or service providers in India.

4. Medical reports

Please share only information relevant to your treatment enquiry. At present, reports may be shared directly through WhatsApp after initial contact.

5. Sharing for coordination

Relevant information may be shared with hospitals, medical teams or service providers involved in the coordination you request.

6. Third-party services

WhatsApp and other third-party services operate under their own privacy practices and terms.

7. Data security

No internet, messaging or electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not send passwords, financial credentials or unrelated documents.

8. Retention and your choices

Information may be retained as reasonably necessary for coordination, records and applicable requirements. You may contact us about correcting information or stopping ongoing use, subject to legitimate retention needs.

9. Children

Information concerning a child should be provided by or with the involvement of a parent, legal guardian or appropriately authorised adult.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Elajme India through the contact or WhatsApp options on our website.
