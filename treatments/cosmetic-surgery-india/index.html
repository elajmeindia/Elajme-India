Cosmetic and reconstructive surgery assessment

Cosmetic procedures

A qualified specialist can assess the patient's goals, medical history and anatomy before discussing appropriate cosmetic options.

Reconstructive surgery

Reconstructive needs after injury, surgery or other conditions require case-specific clinical review and planning.

Safety and recovery

The treating team determines investigations, anaesthesia considerations, recovery expectations and whether a procedure is appropriate.

Elajme India provides coordination and general information. We do not recommend a specific cosmetic procedure, guarantee suitability or promise aesthetic or clinical outcomes.

What to share for an initial review

Useful information may include the main concern or goal, relevant medical and surgical history, current medicines, previous procedure records and any images specifically requested by the treating team. Do not send intimate images unless a verified medical team specifically requires them through an appropriate channel.

1. Share the medical context

Send a brief description of the concern and relevant available records to Elajme India.

2. Coordinate specialist review

We can coordinate submission to an appropriate plastic or reconstructive surgery team for preliminary review.

3. Confirm next steps

Consultation availability, investigations, procedure options, timing, recovery and estimates remain subject to the treating provider's review.

Preparing for plastic and reconstructive surgery review

What the clinical team reviews

Plastic and reconstructive surgery assessment depends on the patient’s goal, anatomy, previous surgery or injury, scars, general health and whether the purpose is reconstruction, functional improvement or cosmetic change.

Records that can help

Send the main concern and treatment goal, relevant photographs where appropriate, previous operation notes, imaging when relevant, medical history, smoking status and current medicines.

The treating team confirms diagnosis, suitability, investigations and treatment after individual review. Elajme India coordinates case submission and communication and does not replace medical advice.
