About Elajme India

Elajme India is an international patient coordination initiative helping patients and families navigate treatment enquiries in India with clear communication and practical support.

Start a treatment enquiry

What we do

We help organise relevant medical information, coordinate case reviews with hospitals or specialist teams, assist with appointments and available treatment estimates, and support practical planning around a treatment visit to India.

Arabic and English communication

International patients can communicate with us in Arabic or English. This helps reduce communication gaps while coordinating enquiries with hospitals and during practical stages of the treatment journey.

How we handle healthcare information

Healthcare decisions can carry serious consequences, so our public content is written to explain coordination and patient-planning topics without presenting general website information as an individual medical recommendation.

Clinical decisions stay with medical professionals

Diagnosis, investigations, treatment recommendations, clinical eligibility and medical follow-up are determined by the relevant qualified doctor or hospital team after reviewing the individual case.

Provider information is descriptive, not a ranking

Doctor and hospital profiles help patients explore available options. Inclusion on Elajme India does not mean a provider is ranked, guaranteed to be suitable for a case, or formally partnered with Elajme India unless that relationship is explicitly stated and verified.

Costs and availability can change

Any treatment estimate, appointment information or hospital response shared during coordination is subject to the provider's confirmation and may change after examination, additional tests or changes in the treatment plan.

We correct information when needed

We aim to keep directory and patient-guidance information accurate and clear. When verified information changes or an error is identified, the relevant page can be corrected without altering the clinical record or opinion supplied by a healthcare provider.

Our content standards

Elajme India separates general patient guidance from clinical advice. We avoid promising treatment outcomes, guaranteed hospital acceptance, guaranteed visa approval, fixed final treatment costs or unsupported claims that a doctor or hospital is the “best” or “No. 1”. Where a page discusses a doctor, hospital or treatment pathway, patients should confirm case-specific details through the treating medical team.

Doctor and hospital information may be based on publicly available provider information and details supplied during legitimate patient coordination. Medical facts are kept general unless they are tied to an identifiable authoritative source or to feedback supplied for the individual patient's case.

Important: Website content is informational and does not replace consultation with a qualified healthcare professional. For urgent or emergency symptoms, patients should seek appropriate local emergency care rather than wait for an international treatment enquiry.

Our approach

Our role is coordination, not medical decision-making. We aim to keep communication clear, avoid pressure to proceed, and help patients understand the next practical step after a hospital or qualified medical professional reviews their case.

Medical note: Elajme India does not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, determine clinical eligibility or guarantee medical outcomes, hospital acceptance or visa approval.
