What to send for an initial review

Medical summary

Diagnosis if known, current medical concern, symptoms and relevant medical history.

Recent reports

Relevant laboratory, pathology, biopsy, discharge or specialist reports where available.

Scans and imaging

MRI, CT, PET-CT, X-ray, ultrasound or other relevant imaging reports and files when available.

Previous treatment

Details of previous surgery, medicines, chemotherapy, radiotherapy or other treatment where applicable.
