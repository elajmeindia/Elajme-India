Spine surgery treatment coordination in India

Elajme India helps international patients coordinate spine-related case reviews with hospitals and specialist teams in India. The appropriate investigation or treatment depends on the individual diagnosis and specialist assessment.

How we support your enquiry

Medical case review

We help organise relevant reports and imaging information for specialist review.

Hospital coordination

We coordinate hospital communication, consultations, appointments and available estimates.

Treatment-visit planning

If you decide to proceed, we assist with practical arrangements connected with your visit to India.

Information that may help with preliminary review

Depending on your condition, the hospital may request recent medical notes, imaging such as MRI or CT reports, previous surgery records and a current medication list. The specialist team will advise what is required for your case.

Medical note: Elajme India provides coordination support and does not diagnose conditions or recommend a particular surgery. Clinical decisions are made by qualified medical professionals.
