Work-related health assessment coordination

Occupational medicine in India for international patients

Elajme India helps international patients coordinate enquiries involving work-related health concerns, occupational exposures and fitness-for-work assessment with appropriate medical teams in India. Clinical conclusions require individual specialist assessment and relevant exposure or employment information.

Request coordination through Elajme India

Assessment considers health and workplace factors

Medical assessment

The treating team reviews symptoms, diagnosis, medical history, previous treatment and investigations to understand the patient’s current health status.

Work and exposure history

Review may include job duties, physical demands, chemicals, dust, noise, repetitive activity, shift patterns or other relevant occupational exposures.

Function and follow-up

Depending on the clinical question, the medical team may assess functional limitations, rehabilitation needs or whether additional specialty evaluation is required.

Elajme India provides international patient coordination and general information. We do not determine employment eligibility, issue workplace certification, diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment or guarantee outcomes.

What to share for an initial review

Useful information may include the medical concern, specialist notes, investigation results, treatment history, job description, relevant workplace exposure history, previous occupational-health assessments and current medicines.

1. Medical records

Send available diagnosis, consultation notes, laboratory tests, imaging, lung or hearing tests when relevant, previous treatment and current medicines.

2. Work information

Describe the occupation, main duties, physical demands, duration of employment and any suspected exposure or work activity associated with the health concern.

3. Clarify the review question

State whether the enquiry concerns a suspected work-related condition, functional assessment, rehabilitation planning or another medical question. The treating team determines the appropriate evaluation after review.
