Medical treatment in India for patients from Oman

Elajme India helps patients and families from Oman coordinate treatment enquiries in India, including medical case review, hospital communication, appointments, available estimates and treatment-visit planning. Arabic and English communication support is available during coordination.

What to prepare for hospital review

Depending on the medical condition, useful information may include consultation notes, laboratory results, imaging reports, pathology reports where relevant, previous treatment records and current medicines. The hospital will confirm the documents required for the individual case.

Planning your visit

After case review, we can help coordinate appointments and practical treatment-visit arrangements. Visa and entry decisions are made by the relevant authorities and cannot be guaranteed by Elajme India.
